How we work these out

For several dice we count all the ways each total can come up: one die has the totals 1 to 6 once each, and each extra die adds its faces to every earlier total. That gives the "ways" column, and dividing by the number of outcomes (6 for one die, 36 for two, 216 for three) gives the chance. Nothing is rounded until the last step.
