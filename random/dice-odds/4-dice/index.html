4 Dice Odds: Chances of Every Total

This page lists the chance of every possible total when you roll 4 fair six-sided dice. The numbers are worked out by counting every one of the 1,296 equally likely outcomes, not estimated by simulation. Use the dice roller to try it.

The headline numbers

  • Lowest total: 4. Highest total: 24.
  • Most likely total: 14, which comes up 11.27% of the time.
  • Average total: 14. Spread (standard deviation): 3.42.
  • Chance of rolling at least the average (14): 55.63%.
  • Rarest totals: 0.077% each.

Where most rolls land

Nine rolls in ten (more precisely, at least 90% of them) give a total between 8 and 20. A total of 4 or more comes up 100.00% of the time. A total of 14 or more comes up 55.63% of the time. A total of 20 or more comes up 5.40% of the time. A total of 24 or more comes up 0.08% of the time. On average you would roll the single most likely total once in about 8.9 rolls, and the very top total once in 1,296 rolls. These are long-run averages: in a short session you will see runs of luck and bad luck, and that is normal.

Compared with 3 dice

Adding a die moves the average from 10.5 to 14 and widens the range of totals from 16 to 21. The likeliest total is 11.27% of rolls with 4 dice against 12.50% with 3 dice, so each extra die makes the result a little more predictable in relative terms.

How to read this

"Ways" counts the ordered outcomes of the dice that give the total, out of 1296 possible. Divide to get the chance. Because the dice are fair and independent, each of the 1296 outcomes has the same chance. A page like this answers practical questions: is it worth chasing a total of 23, or is aiming for 14 much better?
