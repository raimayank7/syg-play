Random tools: fair, fast and private

Dice, coin, numbers, cards, teams and a lucky draw, with exact odds pages that show why randomness works the way it does.

  • Dice Roller Roll one to ten dice with 4 to 100 sides and see each die and the total.
  • Coin Flip Flip a fair coin: heads or tails, with a running count and streak.
  • Random Number Generator Pick random whole numbers in any range, with or without repeats.
  • Random Card Draw Draw cards from a shuffled 52-card deck without replacement.
  • Random Team Picker Split a list of names into random teams of near-equal size.
  • Yes or No Picker A fair yes or no answer for small decisions.
  • Lucky Draw: Pick Random Winners Pick random winners from a list of entries for a free giveaway or class draw.

Learn the odds

Read the exact chances for every dice roll, the surprising coin flip streaks and the birthday problem. Teachers use these pages to show that probability can be counted rather than guessed.

Fair means fair

Everything here runs in your browser with secure random numbers. Read how a fair shuffle works, or the giveaway checklist before you run a draw.

Questions

Are the results really random?

Yes. They come from your browser's cryptographic random number generator, with a method that avoids bias.

Is anything saved or sent?

No. Lists and results stay on your device.

Can I use the draw for a real lottery?

No. The lucky draw is made for free giveaways and fun. Lotteries and betting are regulated, and we do not support them.

Are the odds tables correct?

They are worked out by program from the rules of dice and coins, by counting every outcome, not copied from elsewhere.
