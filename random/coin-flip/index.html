Coin Flip

Flip a fair coin: heads or tails, with a running count and streak.

About this tool

Flip a coin to settle a choice. The coin is fair: heads and tails each have a one in two chance, every time, whatever came before. The page counts heads and tails and shows your current streak.

A streak does not make the next flip more or less likely to match it. People find that hard to believe, which is why the streak page shows how often long runs happen in fair flips. If a friend does not trust the phone, flip together and call it before the result shows.

Is a real coin fair?

A real coin is very close to fair but not perfect: a careful study of hundreds of thousands of flips by researchers found a small bias to land on the side that started facing up. For a decision between friends the difference is far too small to matter.

Questions

Is it really random?

Yes. Results come from your browser's cryptographic random number generator, which is designed for security uses and is far better than needed for a game.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything happens on your device and nothing you type is sent to us.
