Dice Roller

Roll one to ten dice with 4 to 100 sides and see each die and the total.

About this tool

Roll one or more dice online. Choose how many dice and how many sides, from the ordinary six-sided die used in board games to the twenty-sided and hundred-sided dice used in role-playing games. Each roll is made by your browser's secure random number generator and the total is added up for you.

Every die is rolled on its own, so a roll of three dice is three separate fair rolls, not one roll split in three. A six-sided die shows the dot picture; other dice show the number. The page keeps the last eight rolls so you can look back. For the exact chance of any total see the dice odds pages.

Which dice for which game

Board games and most family games use one or two six-sided dice. Role-playing games add four, eight, ten, twelve, twenty and hundred-sided dice. A hundred-sided roll is the same as two ten-sided dice read as tens and units. The odds for every total are on our dice odds pages.

Questions

Is it really random?

Yes. Results come from your browser's cryptographic random number generator, which is designed for security uses and is far better than needed for a game.

Is my data stored?

No. Everything happens on your device and nothing you type is sent to us.
