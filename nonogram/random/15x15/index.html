15 by 15 nonogram

A longer puzzle for patient solvers: a fresh random puzzle each time you press New puzzle.

About this size

This is a hard puzzle of 15 rows and 15 columns, which is 225 squares. It is a longer puzzle for patient solvers. Each puzzle is generated when the page opens and is accepted only if logic can solve it step by step, so there is always exactly one picture to find and never a need to guess.

How to play

Tap or click a square to fill it. Drag to fill several squares in a row. Switch to Mark empty to put a cross on squares you know are empty, which helps you keep track. Check mistakes shows wrong filled squares in red. Show solution fills in the answer. Your progress is saved on this device. New to nonograms? Read how to solve a nonogram.

Other sizes: 5 by 5, 10 by 10.
