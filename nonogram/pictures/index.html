Nonogram pictures

Thirteen drawn pictures, each a 10 by 10 puzzle.

Each picture below was drawn for this site and then checked by a program: only pictures that can be solved by logic, with no guessing, are included. A few of our drawings failed that test and were left out.

Choosing one

Pictures with big solid shapes, such as the heart, the apple and the tent, are the gentlest. The key and the moon have thin lines and need more careful work.
