Why you can trust them

Many nonogram sites make random puzzles that need a guess, which spoils the fun. Here a program rejects any puzzle it cannot solve by logic, and we tested the solver itself against every one of the 65,536 possible 4 by 4 pictures to check that it never marks a wrong square.

Questions

Do I have to guess?

No. Every puzzle on this site is checked by a solver that uses logic only, line by line. If it cannot finish a puzzle, the puzzle is not used.

Is it free?

Yes. There is no login and nothing to download.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Tap to fill and drag across squares to fill several at once. A switch lets you cross out empty squares.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, on this device only, in your browser.
