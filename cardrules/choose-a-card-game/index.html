Choose a card game

The right card game depends on who is playing, how old they are and how long you have. This table lists all twelve games on the site with those facts, and the advice below helps you choose.

For young children

Snap, Go Fish and Old Maid need no strategy and can be learned in a minute. War needs no decisions at all. They work from about age four or five, with help.

For two players

Gin Rummy and War are made for two. Go Fish and Snap also work well with two. Crazy Eights works with two players, with a bigger hand (7 cards each).

For a party of four

Hearts, Spades and Whist are made for four. Spades and Whist are played in partnerships, which makes them sociable; Hearts is every player for themselves.

For a big group

Bluff, Snap and Crazy Eights work with larger groups (add a second deck over six players).

Solo

Klondike Solitaire is the classic game for one.
