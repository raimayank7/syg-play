Card game rules in plain English

Twelve well-known games, each with the deal, the play, the scoring, common variations and tips.

  • Go Fish 2 to 6 players, matching
  • Old Maid 2 to 8 players, matching
  • Snap 2 to 6 players, reaction
  • War 2 players, luck
  • Crazy Eights 2 to 7 players, shedding
  • Hearts 4 (3 to 5 variants exist) players, trick-taking
  • Spades 4 in two partnerships players, trick-taking
  • Whist 4 in two partnerships players, trick-taking
  • Gin Rummy 2 players, rummy
  • Indian Rummy (13 Cards) 2 to 6 players, rummy
  • Bluff (Cheat) 3 to 10 players, bluffing
  • Klondike Solitaire 1 players, solitaire

How to use this site

Pick a game from the list, or use the chooser to find one for your group by players, age and time. Each page has the goal, the set-up, the steps of play, the scoring, variations and tips. Words you do not know are in the glossary.

Rules differ between families, so read our note on house rules before the first deal.

Questions

Are the rules the official ones?

Most card games have no single official rulebook. We describe the version most groups play and say where groups differ.

Do I need a special deck?

No. Every game here uses a standard 52-card deck, with two decks or jokers where we say so.

Is this about gambling?

No. We cover family and social games. Check local law if you play for money.
