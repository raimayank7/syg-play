Card game glossary

Card games have their own words. This glossary explains the ones used on this site, in plain English.

WordMeaning
SuitOne of the four groups of cards: spades, hearts, diamonds and clubs.
RankThe value of a card within its suit: 2 up to 10, then jack, queen, king and ace (ace is high or low depending on the game).
TrickOne round of a trick-taking game in which each player plays one card and the highest card wins them all.
TrumpA suit that beats the other suits for the hand. A trump card beats any card of another suit.
Follow suitTo play a card of the same suit as the card led, if you have one.
LeadTo play the first card of a trick.
DiscardTo play a card of a different suit that is not trump, or to put a card on the discard pile.
StockThe pile of face-down cards from which players draw.
Discard pileThe face-up pile where played cards are put.
MeldA group of cards that scores or counts in rummy games: a set or a run.
SetThree or four cards of the same rank.
Sequence or runThree or more cards of the same suit in order.
DeadwoodCards in your hand that are not part of a meld, and so count against you.
BookA set of four cards of one rank in Go Fish, or the first six tricks in Whist.
BagAn extra trick won above your bid in Spades.
NilA bid to win no tricks.
Wild cardA card that can stand for any other card in some games, such as the eight in Crazy Eights.
Shooting the moonTaking all the penalty cards in Hearts, which reverses the scores.
