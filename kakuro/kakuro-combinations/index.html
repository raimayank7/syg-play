Kakuro combinations cheat sheet

In Kakuro the digits in a block are all different and add up to the total. For any total and any number of squares there are only a few possible sets of digits, and some totals have just one. Knowing these sets is what makes Kakuro solvable. The lists below are generated by listing every set of different digits from 1 to 9 and grouping them by total, so there are no typing mistakes.

The ones worth memorising

For two squares: 3 is 1+2, 4 is 1+3, 16 is 7+9 and 17 is 8+9. For three squares: 6 is 1+2+3, 7 is 1+2+4, 23 is 6+8+9 and 24 is 7+8+9. For four squares: 10 is 1+2+3+4, 11 is 1+2+3+5, 29 is 5+7+8+9 and 30 is 6+7+8+9. Each is the only way to make that total, so you can pencil in those digits straight away.
