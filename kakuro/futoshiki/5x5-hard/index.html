Futoshiki 5 by 5 (Hard)

Numbers 1 to 5 with inequality signs, few signs and almost no starting numbers.

About this puzzle

This is a hard puzzle on a 5 by 5 grid: each row and column holds the numbers 1 to 5 once, and the signs between squares show which number is smaller. It has few signs and almost no starting numbers. The puzzle is made when the page opens, and a program checks that exactly one grid fits the signs and numbers.

How to play

Tap a square, then a number. Squares that break a rule show in red. Check says whether any digit is wrong. New puzzle makes another. Read how to solve Futoshiki if you are new to it.

Other levels: Easy, Medium.
