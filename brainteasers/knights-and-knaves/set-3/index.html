Knights and knaves puzzles, set 3

On an island, knights always tell the truth and knaves always lie. Each puzzle below gives what some islanders say. Work out who is a knight and who is a knave. Every puzzle has exactly one answer, which our program checked by trying every possible assignment, and the answer is hidden until you press the button.

Puzzle 3.1 (3 people)

  • Asha says: “Chen is a knave.”
  • Bilal says: “Either Chen or Asha is a knight, but not both.”
  • Chen says: “Bilal is a knight.”
Show the answer

Asha is a knave, Bilal is a knight, Chen is a knight.

How to solve these

Pick one islander and suppose they are a knight. Then their statement is true, which fixes others. Follow the chain; if you reach a contradiction, they must be a knave. With two or three people there are only four or eight cases, so you can always try them all. The tables in the answers show every case.
