Tower of Hanoi with 2 discs

Three pegs, 2 discs of different sizes stacked largest at the bottom on peg A. Move the whole stack to peg C, one disc at a time, never placing a bigger disc on a smaller one. With 2 discs the fewest possible moves is 3, which is 2 to the power 2 minus 1.

The numbers

  • Fewest moves: 3.
  • At one move a second, that is 0.1 minutes.
  • The smallest disc moves 2 times, every other move.
  • The first move puts the smallest disc on peg B.

Every move

MoveDiscFromTo
1disc 1AB
2disc 2AC
3disc 1BC

How to solve it

To move 2 discs from A to C, move the top 1 discs to B, move the largest disc to C, then move those 1 discs from B onto C. Each of those two sub-moves is the same puzzle with one disc fewer, which is why the count doubles and gains one each time: M(2) = 2 × M(1) + 1 = 3. The same idea gives a rule you can follow without thinking: move the smallest disc one peg in the same direction every second move (it goes A, B, C, A, ... with 2 discs), and between those moves make the only other legal move.

We checked this page by program: the move list was played out on a model of the pegs and every move was legal and the final stack was complete.

Other sizes: 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.
