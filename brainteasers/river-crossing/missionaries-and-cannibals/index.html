The missionaries and cannibals puzzle

Three missionaries and three cannibals must cross a river in a boat that holds two. If cannibals ever outnumber the missionaries on either bank where missionaries are present, the missionaries are eaten. How do they all get across? (The puzzle comes from the 1800s and is a standard exercise in search, so you will find it in many textbooks.)

The table is worked out by the same search. Four of each with a two-person boat cannot be done: the search finds no safe route. Making the boat hold three makes it possible in 9 crossings.

How the search works

Each situation is described by how many of each group are on the left bank and which side the boat is on. A move is allowed if the boat is not overloaded and no bank is unsafe afterwards. We explore from the start, always trying the shortest routes first, until the "everyone on the right" situation is reached. The first time it is reached is by definition the shortest solution. If the search runs out of situations without reaching it, the puzzle is impossible.
