Bridge and torch puzzle: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6

6 people must cross a narrow bridge at night. They have one torch and the bridge holds at most two people at a time. Each person walks at their own speed, and a pair walks at the slower person's speed. Their crossing times are 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 minutes. The torch must travel with every crossing, so someone has to bring it back. What is the shortest total time?

The answer: 22 minutes

A search over every possible arrangement (who is on which bank and where the torch is) finds that 22 minutes is the least possible. The obvious plan, where the fastest person takes each of the others across in turn and walks back with the torch, takes 24 minutes, so it is 2 minutes slower than the best.

The pattern behind the best answer

The best plan always moves the two slowest people together so the slowest time is paid only once. For each pair of slowest people there are two ways to do it: either the two fastest ferry them (costing 1 + 2×2 + 6 = 11) or the fastest escorts each (costing 2×1 + 5 + 6 = 13), and the best total is the cheaper of the two each round. Here the program checked that this rule gives 22, the same as the full search.

Try your own times

Change one time and the best plan can change completely. Compare the other sets: 1, 2, 5, 10, 1, 2, 5, 8, 1, 3, 4, 5, 2, 3, 7, 9, 12.
