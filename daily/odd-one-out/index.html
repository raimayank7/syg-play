Daily odd one out

About this puzzle

A grid of identical shapes hides one that is slightly different: a little lighter, a little bigger or a little tilted. Tap it to win. On easy days the difference is large, on hard days it is subtle.

How to play

  1. Look at the whole grid first, without staring at one place.
  2. Move your eyes in a slow zigzag across the rows.
  3. On a phone, tilt the screen slightly or reduce screen glare if shades look alike.
  4. The puzzle needs sight of shade and size; if that is hard for you, the number puzzles may suit you better.

A tip

See tips for odd one out for how to scan a grid.

Levels through the week

On easy days the grid is 4 by 4 and the odd shape is clearly different. On hard days it is 6 by 6 and the difference is small, so you need patience. The kind of difference (shade, size or tilt) changes from day to day.

Questions

Is it a colour test?

No. It is a fun puzzle, not a medical test. Some days the difference is in size or tilt, not shade.

Can I use the keyboard?

Yes. Tab to a shape and press Enter or Space.

Why is it harder on a phone outdoors?

Bright light on the screen makes shades hard to compare. Turn up the brightness or move into shade.
