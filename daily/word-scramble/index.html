Daily word scramble

About this puzzle

Five words with their letters mixed up. The first letter of each is shown as a hint. Type the word, and when it is right it locks in with a tick. All five make a solved puzzle.

How to play

  1. Look at the scrambled letters and find the word endings first: ING, ED, ER, TION.
  2. Try the first letter plus common pairs: TH, CH, ST.
  3. Say the letters aloud in different orders.
  4. Any word made from the same letters that is in our list is accepted.

A tip

Our anagram tips have more ideas.

Levels through the week

Easy days use four and five letter words. Mid-week uses five and six letters. At the weekend the words are six to eight letters long, which are harder to unscramble because there are many more orders of the letters.

Questions

What if I find a different word?

Any word in our list that uses the same letters is accepted, for example two words that are anagrams of each other.

Are the words hard?

They are everyday words: names of things, places and actions. The first letter is shown as a hint.

Do I need capital letters?

No. Type in lower case or upper case.
