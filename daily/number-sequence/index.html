Daily number sequence

About this puzzle

A sequence is a list of numbers that follows a rule. You see the first five and choose the sixth from four options. When you answer correctly, the rule is explained, so every puzzle teaches you a pattern.

How to play

  1. Look at the gaps between neighbouring numbers.
  2. If the gaps are equal, add. If the numbers are doubling or tripling, multiply.
  3. If the gaps grow by the same amount each time, think of squares or triangular numbers.
  4. Choose the answer; a wrong choice does not end the puzzle.

A tip

The six pages on the sequence types show each kind of rule with worked examples.

Levels through the week

Monday and Tuesday sequences are only arithmetic or multiplying ones. Mid-week adds squares, triangular numbers and the add-two-terms rule. The weekend can also use alternating steps, cubes and prime numbers, which take more thought.

Questions

What if two answers seem right?

Only one is right by the rule we use. When you answer correctly, the rule is explained, so you can see why.

Are the sequences hard?

They get harder through the week. Monday has only the simplest rules.

Why do I get a second try?

A wrong choice does not end the puzzle. Try again.
