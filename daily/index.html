A five-minute habit

Daily puzzles work best as a small routine. The levels rise from very easy on Monday to hard at the weekend, so the week has a shape: ease in on Monday, stretch yourself by Friday. Everyone sees the same puzzles on the same day, so you can compare notes with friends, family or a class. Teachers can read ideas for the classroom and parents ideas for the family.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. No login, no download. Your streak is kept in your own browser.

When do the puzzles change?

At midnight by your device’s clock. Everyone with the same date sees the same puzzles.

Can every puzzle be solved?

Yes. The number puzzles are built backwards from an answer, and the other puzzles follow rules we check.

What happens if I miss a day?

Your streak goes back to zero and you start again. The puzzles of the next day are new.
