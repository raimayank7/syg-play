Daily Puzzles

How streaks work

A streak is the number of days in a row on which you solved at least one puzzle. It is meant to help you build a small daily habit, not to make you anxious.

What counts

  • Solving any one of the four puzzles counts for the day.
  • The day changes at midnight by your device’s clock.
  • If you have not solved a puzzle yet today, your streak still shows the days up to yesterday, so you can keep it going until midnight.

If you miss a day

The streak goes back to zero. That is fine: start again. The puzzles on the next day are new, and nothing is lost except the number.

Where it is kept

Your streak is stored in your browser on this device, in local storage. It is not sent to us, we do not know who you are, and there is no account. If you clear your browser data, change browser, or use a private window, the streak will start again from zero. Two devices have separate streaks.

Why small streaks help

Habits are built by doing something small, at the same time, every day. A puzzle that takes five minutes is easy to fit in, which is why streaks of small tasks last longer than plans for big ones. The streak counter is only a reminder: the real benefit is the practice.

A healthy habit

Ten minutes a day is plenty. Pick a regular time, such as after breakfast or on the bus, and do the puzzles then. Many people find that the number puzzle on a hard day is a good way to wake up the brain.

Questions

  • Can I fix my streak if I forget? No, a streak is only the honest count of days you solved a puzzle.
  • Does the streak sync between my phone and computer? No. Each browser keeps its own, because nothing is sent to us.
  • How many days are remembered? Up to the last 400 days.
  • Is there a leaderboard? No. There are no accounts, so no leaderboard and no comparing with strangers.
