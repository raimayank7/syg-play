About the data and counting rules

This site covers the men's FIFA World Cup from 1930 to 2022. The match data comes from the openfootball project, which publishes World Cup results as plain text files that it has put in the public domain (CC0 1.0). We read those files with a program and checked the result against well-known totals: 964 matches and 2,720 goals in all, and the match and goal total of each of the 22 tournaments. All 22 matched exactly before any page was built.

Counting rules

  • Goals include extra time. Goals in a penalty shoot-out are not goals.
  • A match decided on penalties counts as a draw in team records, as FIFA does.
  • Records of teams that changed name or country are merged in the usual way: West Germany into Germany, the Soviet Union into Russia, Czechoslovakia into the Czech Republic, Yugoslavia and Serbia and Montenegro into Serbia, Zaire into DR Congo, the Dutch East Indies into Indonesia. East Germany is kept separate.
  • 1950 had no final: its last stage was a round of four teams, and Uruguay won it.
  • 1930 had no third-place match, so third and fourth place are left blank.
  • Only matches in the finals tournaments count; qualifying matches are not included.

What is not here

Scorers, cards, line-ups and the women's World Cup are not covered. If you find a mistake, please tell us. This site is independent: it has no connection with FIFA and uses no FIFA logos, mascots or photos.
