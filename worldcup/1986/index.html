FIFA World Cup 1986

The 13th World Cup was hosted by Mexico and played by 24 teams over 52 matches. Argentina won the tournament beating West Germany in the final (3-2). France finished third and Belgium fourth.

The numbers

  • 52 matches and 132 goals, an average of 2.54 goals a match (the average for all World Cups from 1930 to 2022 is 2.82).
  • Highest-scoring match: Denmark 6-1 Uruguay, with 7 goals.
  • Biggest margin: Soviet Union 6-0 Hungary, by 6 goals.
  • 14 matches ended level after 90 minutes or extra time; 3 matches decided on penalties.
  • 4 matches finished with no goals.

The road to the title

Argentina played 7 matches on the way to the title, scoring 14 goals and conceding 5. In the group stage beat South Korea 3-1; in the group stage drew with Italy 1-1; in the group stage beat Bulgaria 2-0; in the round of 16 beat Uruguay 1-0; in the quarter-finals beat England 2-1; in the semi-finals beat Belgium 2-0; in the final beat West Germany 3-2.
