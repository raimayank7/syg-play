France vs Italy at the World Cup

France and Italy have met 5 times at the World Cup, between 1938 and 2006. France won 1, Italy won 2 and 2 ended level (a match decided on penalties counts as level). The goals are 5 for France and 6 for Italy. Italy leads the series.

The series at a glance

The first meeting was in 1938, in the quarter-finals: France 1-3 Italy. The most recent was in 2006, in the final: Italy 1-1 a.e.t. (5-3 on penalties) France. 1 of the meetings came in group or league stages and 4 in knockout rounds, where France won 1 and Italy won 1. The most common result was 3-1, which happened 1 time. Across all meetings the teams scored 2.2 goals a match on average, against 2.82 for all World Cup matches. The meetings were played at tournaments hosted by Argentina, France, Germany, Mexico.

In order: 1938 (quarter-finals): France 1-3 Italy. 1978 (group stage): Italy 2-1 France. 1986 (round of 16): Italy 0-2 France. 1998 (quarter-finals): Italy 0-0 after extra time (3-4 on penalties) France. 2006 (final): Italy 1-1 after extra time (5-3 on penalties) France.
