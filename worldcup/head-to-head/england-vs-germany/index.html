England vs Germany at the World Cup

England and Germany have met 5 times at the World Cup, between 1966 and 2010. England won 1, Germany won 2 and 2 ended level (a match decided on penalties counts as level). The goals are 8 for England and 10 for Germany. Germany leads the series.

The series at a glance

The first meeting was in 1966, in the final: England 4-2 a.e.t. West Germany. The most recent was in 2010, in the round of 16: Germany 4-1 England. 1 of the meetings came in group or league stages and 4 in knockout rounds, where England won 1 and Germany won 2. The most common result was 4-2, which happened 1 time. Across all meetings the teams scored 3.6 goals a match on average, against 2.82 for all World Cup matches. The meetings were played at tournaments hosted by England, Italy, Mexico, South Africa, Spain.

In order: 1966 (final): England 4-2 after extra time West Germany. 1970 (quarter-finals): West Germany 3-2 after extra time England. 1982 (second group stage): West Germany 0-0 England. 1990 (semi-finals): West Germany 1-1 after extra time (4-3 on penalties) England. 2010 (round of 16): Germany 4-1 England.
