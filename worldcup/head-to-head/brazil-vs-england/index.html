Brazil vs England at the World Cup

Brazil and England have met 4 times at the World Cup, between 1958 and 2002. Brazil won 3, England won 0 and 1 ended level (a match decided on penalties counts as level). The goals are 6 for Brazil and 2 for England. Brazil leads the series.

The series at a glance

The first meeting was in 1958, in the group stage: Brazil 0-0 England. The most recent was in 2002, in the quarter-finals: England 1-2 Brazil. 2 of the meetings came in group or league stages and 2 in knockout rounds, where Brazil won 2 and England won 0. The most common result was 0-0, which happened 1 time. Across all meetings the teams scored 2.0 goals a match on average, against 2.82 for all World Cup matches. The meetings were played at tournaments hosted by Chile, Mexico, South Korea and Japan, Sweden.

In order: 1958 (group stage): Brazil 0-0 England. 1962 (quarter-finals): Brazil 3-1 England. 1970 (group stage): England 0-1 Brazil. 2002 (quarter-finals): England 1-2 Brazil.
