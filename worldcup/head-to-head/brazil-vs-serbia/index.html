Brazil vs Serbia at the World Cup

Brazil and Serbia have met 6 times at the World Cup, between 1930 and 2022. Brazil won 3, Serbia won 1 and 2 ended level (a match decided on penalties counts as level). The goals are 8 for Brazil and 3 for Serbia. Brazil leads the series.

The series at a glance

The first meeting was in 1930, in the group stage: Yugoslavia 2-1 Brazil. The most recent was in 2022, in the group stage: Brazil 2-0 Serbia. 6 of the meetings came in group or league stages and 0 in knockout rounds. The most common result was 2-0, which happened 3 times. Across all meetings the teams scored 1.8 goals a match on average, against 2.82 for all World Cup matches. The meetings were played at tournaments hosted by Brazil, Qatar, Russia, Switzerland, Uruguay, West Germany.

In order: 1930 (group stage): Yugoslavia 2-1 Brazil. 1950 (group stage): Brazil 2-0 Yugoslavia. 1954 (group stage): Brazil 1-1 after extra time Yugoslavia. 1974 (group stage): Brazil 0-0 Yugoslavia. 2018 (group stage): Serbia 0-2 Brazil. 2022 (group stage): Brazil 2-0 Serbia.
