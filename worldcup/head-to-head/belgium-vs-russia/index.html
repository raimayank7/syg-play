Belgium vs Russia at the World Cup

Belgium and Russia have met 5 times at the World Cup, between 1970 and 2014. Belgium won 3, Russia won 2 and 0 ended level (a match decided on penalties counts as level). The goals are 9 for Belgium and 10 for Russia. Belgium leads the series.

The series at a glance

The first meeting was in 1970, in the group stage: Soviet Union 4-1 Belgium. The most recent was in 2014, in the group stage: Belgium 1-0 Russia. 4 of the meetings came in group or league stages and 1 in knockout rounds, where Belgium won 1 and Russia won 0. The most common result was 1-0, which happened 2 times. Across all meetings the teams scored 3.8 goals a match on average, against 2.82 for all World Cup matches. The meetings were played at tournaments hosted by Brazil, Mexico, South Korea and Japan, Spain.

In order: 1970 (group stage): Soviet Union 4-1 Belgium. 1982 (second group stage): Belgium 0-1 Soviet Union. 1986 (round of 16): Soviet Union 3-4 after extra time Belgium. 2002 (group stage): Belgium 3-2 Russia. 2014 (group stage): Belgium 1-0 Russia.
