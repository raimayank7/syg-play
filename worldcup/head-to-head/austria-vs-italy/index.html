Austria vs Italy at the World Cup

Austria and Italy have met 4 times at the World Cup, between 1934 and 1998. Austria won 0, Italy won 4 and 0 ended level (a match decided on penalties counts as level). The goals are 1 for Austria and 5 for Italy. Italy leads the series.

The series at a glance

The first meeting was in 1934, in the semi-finals: Italy 1-0 Austria. The most recent was in 1998, in the group stage: Italy 2-1 Austria. 3 of the meetings came in group or league stages and 1 in knockout rounds, where Austria won 0 and Italy won 1.
