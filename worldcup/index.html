Why trust the numbers

We read the open match files by program and compared each tournament's match and goal totals with the official figures before building any page. They matched for all 22 tournaments. Team records match widely published ones, for example Brazil with 114 matches, 76 wins, 19 draws and 19 defeats. Read about the data.

Questions

Where does the data come from?

From the openfootball project, whose World Cup files are in the public domain (CC0). We checked them against the official totals of 964 matches and 2,720 goals.

Is it official?

No. This site has no connection with FIFA.

Is the 2026 World Cup included?

Only on its own page, because those results have not been checked in the same way.
