FIFA World Cup 1934

The 2nd World Cup was hosted by Italy and played by 16 teams over 17 matches. Italy won the tournament beating Czechoslovakia in the final (2-1 a.e.t.). Germany finished third and Austria fourth.

The numbers

  • 17 matches and 70 goals, an average of 4.12 goals a match (the average for all World Cups from 1930 to 2022 is 2.82).
  • Highest-scoring match: Italy 7-1 USA, with 8 goals.
  • Biggest margin: Italy 7-1 USA, by 6 goals.
  • 1 match ended level after 90 minutes or extra time; none went to a penalty shoot-out.
  • 0 matches finished with no goals.

The road to the title

Italy played 5 matches on the way to the title, scoring 12 goals and conceding 3. In the round of 16 beat USA 7-1; in the quarter-finals drew with Spain 1-1; in the quarter-finals beat Spain 1-0; in the semi-finals beat Austria 1-0; in the final beat Czechoslovakia 2-1.
