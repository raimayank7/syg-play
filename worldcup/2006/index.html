FIFA World Cup 2006

The 18th World Cup was hosted by Germany and played by 32 teams over 64 matches. Italy won the tournament beating France in the final (1-1 a.e.t. (5-3 on penalties)). Germany finished third and Portugal fourth.

The numbers

  • 64 matches and 147 goals, an average of 2.30 goals a match (the average for all World Cups from 1930 to 2022 is 2.82).
  • Highest-scoring match: Argentina 6-0 Serbia and Montenegro, with 6 goals.
  • Biggest margin: Argentina 6-0 Serbia and Montenegro, by 6 goals.
  • 15 matches ended level after 90 minutes or extra time; 4 matches decided on penalties.
  • 7 matches finished with no goals.

The road to the title

Italy played 7 matches on the way to the title, scoring 12 goals and conceding 2. In the group stage beat Ghana 2-0; in the group stage drew with USA 1-1; in the group stage beat Czech Republic 2-0; in the round of 16 beat Australia 1-0; in the quarter-finals beat Ukraine 3-0; in the semi-finals beat Germany 2-0; in the final drew with France 1-1 and won on penalties.
