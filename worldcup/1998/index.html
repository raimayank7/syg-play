FIFA World Cup 1998

The 16th World Cup was hosted by France and played by 32 teams over 64 matches. France won the tournament beating Brazil in the final (0-3). Croatia finished third and Netherlands fourth.

The numbers

  • 64 matches and 171 goals, an average of 2.67 goals a match (the average for all World Cups from 1930 to 2022 is 2.82).
  • Highest-scoring match: Spain 6-1 Bulgaria, with 7 goals.
  • Biggest margin: Spain 6-1 Bulgaria, by 5 goals.
  • 19 matches ended level after 90 minutes or extra time; 3 matches decided on penalties.
  • 4 matches finished with no goals.

The road to the title

France played 7 matches on the way to the title, scoring 15 goals and conceding 2. In the group stage beat South Africa 3-0; in the group stage beat Saudi Arabia 4-0; in the group stage beat Denmark 2-1; in the round of 16 beat Paraguay 1-0; in the quarter-finals drew with Italy 0-0 and won on penalties; in the semi-finals beat Croatia 2-1; in the final beat Brazil 3-0.
