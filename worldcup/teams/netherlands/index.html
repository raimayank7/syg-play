Netherlands at the World Cup

Netherlands has played in 11 World Cups (1934, 1938, 1974, 1978, 1990, 1994, 1998, 2006, 2010, 2014, 2022). In 55 matches it won 30, drew 14 and lost 11, scoring 96 goals and conceding 52 (a goal difference of +44). Its best result is runners-up in 1974, 1978, 2010.

Patterns

The longest winning run is 6 matches in a row, and the longest run without defeat is 12 matches. In 55 matches the team kept a clean sheet 21 times and scored three or more goals 10 times, and it failed to score 11 times. Its first World Cup was in 1934 (Italy) and its latest, up to 2022, was in 2022 (Qatar). In knockout matches it has won 9 of 22.

Penalty shoot-outs

Netherlands has been in 4 penalty shoot-outs, winning 1 and losing 3.
