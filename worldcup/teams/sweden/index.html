Sweden at the World Cup

Sweden has played in 12 World Cups (1934, 1938, 1950, 1958, 1970, 1974, 1978, 1990, 1994, 2002, 2006, 2018). In 51 matches it won 19, drew 13 and lost 19, scoring 80 goals and conceding 73 (a goal difference of +7). Its best result is runners-up in 1958.

Patterns

The longest winning run is 3 matches in a row, and the longest run without defeat is 6 matches. In 51 matches the team kept a clean sheet 14 times and scored three or more goals 11 times, and it failed to score 11 times.
