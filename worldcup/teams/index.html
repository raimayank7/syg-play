All World Cup teams

Eighty national teams have played in the men's World Cup from 1930 to 2022. The table orders them by matches played. Records follow the usual FIFA convention: West Germany counts as Germany, the Soviet Union as Russia, Czechoslovakia as the Czech Republic, Yugoslavia and Serbia and Montenegro as Serbia, Zaire as DR Congo and the Dutch East Indies as Indonesia. East Germany is a separate team.
