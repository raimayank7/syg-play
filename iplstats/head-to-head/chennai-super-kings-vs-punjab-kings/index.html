The story in numbers

Chennai Super Kings have won 48% of the completed meetings and Punjab Kings 52%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 168.5 for Chennai Super Kings and 165.9 for Punjab Kings. The biggest win in runs by Chennai Super Kings was by 97 runs. The biggest win in runs by Punjab Kings was by 54 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-04-03 at MA Chidambaram Stadium, won by Punjab Kings by 5 wickets.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2015, and 2015 onwards), Chennai Super Kings won 8 of 15 early meetings and 8 of 18 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has stayed fairly steady.
