The story in numbers

Royal Challengers Bengaluru have won 44% of the completed meetings and Sunrisers Hyderabad 56%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 166.4 for Royal Challengers Bengaluru and 173.3 for Sunrisers Hyderabad. The biggest win in runs by Royal Challengers Bengaluru was by 67 runs. The biggest win in runs by Sunrisers Hyderabad was by 118 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-05-22 at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal, won by Sunrisers Hyderabad by 55 runs.

Batting first or chasing

In this fixture the team batting first won 15 of the 27 matches with a result (56%). The toss winner chose to field in 19 of the 27 matches. The longest winning run is 2 matches for Royal Challengers Bengaluru and 4 for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2019, and 2019 onwards), Royal Challengers Bengaluru won 5 of 12 early meetings and 7 of 15 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has stayed fairly steady.
