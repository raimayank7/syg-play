The story in numbers

Mumbai Indians have won 58% of the completed meetings and Sunrisers Hyderabad 42%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 164.0 for Mumbai Indians and 164.0 for Sunrisers Hyderabad. The biggest win in runs by Mumbai Indians was by 42 runs. The biggest win in runs by Sunrisers Hyderabad was by 85 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-04-29 at Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai, won by Sunrisers Hyderabad by 6 wickets.

Batting first or chasing

In this fixture the team batting first won 12 of the 26 matches with a result (46%). The toss winner chose to field in 15 of the 26 matches. The longest winning run is 3 matches for Mumbai Indians and 3 for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2019, and 2019 onwards), Mumbai Indians won 5 of 12 early meetings and 10 of 14 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has shifted.

Grounds and standout players

The most common venues for this fixture are Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal (10), Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai (10), Sharjah Cricket Stadium (2). The players most often named player of the match in these meetings are KA Pollard (2), JJ Bumrah (2), Rashid Khan (2).
