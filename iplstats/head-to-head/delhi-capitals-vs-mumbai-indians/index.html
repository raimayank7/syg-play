The story in numbers

Delhi Capitals have won 45% of the completed meetings and Mumbai Indians 55%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 153.7 for Delhi Capitals and 168.1 for Mumbai Indians. The biggest win in runs by Delhi Capitals was by 37 runs. The biggest win in runs by Mumbai Indians was by 146 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-04-04 at Arun Jaitley Stadium, won by Delhi Capitals by 6 wickets.

Batting first or chasing

In this fixture the team batting first won 20 of the 38 matches with a result (53%). The toss winner chose to field in 26 of the 38 matches. The longest winning run is 3 matches for Delhi Capitals and 5 for Mumbai Indians.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2017, and 2017 onwards), Delhi Capitals won 9 of 18 early meetings and 8 of 20 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has stayed fairly steady.
