The story in numbers

Kolkata Knight Riders have won 32% of the completed meetings and Mumbai Indians 68%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 151.8 for Kolkata Knight Riders and 157.3 for Mumbai Indians. The biggest win in runs by Kolkata Knight Riders was by 52 runs. The biggest win in runs by Mumbai Indians was by 102 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-05-20 at Eden Gardens, Kolkata, won by Kolkata Knight Riders by 4 wickets.

Batting first or chasing

In this fixture the team batting first won 16 of the 37 matches with a result (43%). The toss winner chose to field in 27 of the 37 matches. The longest winning run is 3 matches for Kolkata Knight Riders and 8 for Mumbai Indians.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2017, and 2017 onwards), Kolkata Knight Riders won 5 of 18 early meetings and 7 of 19 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has stayed fairly steady.
