The story in numbers

Chennai Super Kings have won 49% of the completed meetings and Mumbai Indians 51%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 157.5 for Chennai Super Kings and 156.9 for Mumbai Indians. The biggest win in runs by Chennai Super Kings was by 103 runs. The biggest win in runs by Mumbai Indians was by 60 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-05-02 at MA Chidambaram Stadium, won by Chennai Super Kings by 8 wickets.

Batting first or chasing

In this fixture the team batting first won 18 of the 41 matches with a result (44%). The toss winner chose to field in 26 of the 41 matches. The longest winning run is 4 matches for Chennai Super Kings and 5 for Mumbai Indians.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2015, and 2015 onwards), Chennai Super Kings won 9 of 18 early meetings and 11 of 23 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has stayed fairly steady.
