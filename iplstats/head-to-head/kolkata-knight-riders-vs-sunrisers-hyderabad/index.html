The story in numbers

Kolkata Knight Riders have won 66% of the completed meetings and Sunrisers Hyderabad 34%. The two teams’ average innings against each other is 158.2 for Kolkata Knight Riders and 162.5 for Sunrisers Hyderabad. The biggest win in runs by Kolkata Knight Riders was by 80 runs. The biggest win in runs by Sunrisers Hyderabad was by 110 runs. The most recent meeting was on 2026-05-03 at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal, won by Kolkata Knight Riders by 7 wickets.

In this fixture the team batting first won 17 of the 32 matches with a result (53%). The toss winner chose to field in 19 of the 32 matches. The longest winning run is 5 matches for Kolkata Knight Riders and 2 for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Then and now

Splitting the meetings in the middle (before 2019, and 2019 onwards), Kolkata Knight Riders won 9 of 15 early meetings and 12 of 17 later ones, so the balance of the rivalry has stayed fairly steady.
