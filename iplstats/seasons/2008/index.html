Points are worked out as the IPL Playing Conditions 2026 (clause 16.10.1) give them: 2 for a win (a tied match decided by a Super Over counts as a win for the winner) and 1 for each team in a match with no result. The conditions separate teams on equal points by wins, then net run rate, then wickets per ball. We order by points then wins and do not show net run rate, so teams level on both are in alphabetical order; check an official table for the exact order of tied teams. Season in numbers

The 2008 season had 58 matches (55 in the league stage and 3 in the play-offs), with 17,937 runs scored in all: an average of 309.3 per match for the two teams together, or about 154.6 per team innings. The highest innings was 240 by Chennai Super Kings against Kings XI Punjab. There were 623 sixes and 1703 fours, which works out at 10.7 sixes a match. Counting only completed matches, the side batting first won 24 times and the side chasing won 34 times.
