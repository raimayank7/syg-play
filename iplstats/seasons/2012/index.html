The conditions separate teams on equal points by wins, then net run rate, then wickets per ball. Season in numbers

The 2012 season had 74 matches (70 in the league stage and 4 in the play-offs), with 22,453 runs scored in all: an average of 303.4 per match for the two teams together, or about 151.7 per team innings. The highest innings was 222 by Chennai Super Kings against Delhi Daredevils. There were 733 sixes and 1911 fours, which works out at 9.9 sixes a match. Counting only completed matches, the side batting first won 34 times and the side chasing won 40 times.
