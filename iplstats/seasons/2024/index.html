Points are worked out as the IPL Playing Conditions 2026 (clause 16.10.1) give them: 2 for a win (a tied match decided by a Super Over counts as a win for the winner) and 1 for each team in a match with no result. The conditions separate teams on equal points by wins, then net run rate, then wickets per ball. We order by points then wins and do not show net run rate, so teams level on both are in alphabetical order; check an official table for the exact order of tied teams.

Season in numbers

The 2024 season had 71 matches (67 in the league stage and 4 in the play-offs), with 25,971 runs scored in all: an average of 365.8 per match for the two teams together, or about 182.9 per team innings. The highest innings was 287 by Sunrisers Hyderabad against Royal Challengers Bengaluru. There were 1261 sixes and 2174 fours, which works out at 17.8 sixes a match. Counting only completed matches, the side batting first won 35 times and the side chasing won 36 times.
