Season in numbers

The 2010 season had 60 matches (56 in the league stage and 4 in the play-offs), with 18,864 runs scored in all: an average of 314.4 per match for the two teams together, or about 157.2 per team innings. The highest innings was 246 by Chennai Super Kings against Rajasthan Royals. There were 585 sixes and 1708 fours, which works out at 9.8 sixes a match. Counting only completed matches, the side batting first won 31 times and the side chasing won 28 times.
