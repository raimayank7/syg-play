Points are worked out as the IPL Playing Conditions 2026 (clause 16.10.1) give them: 2 for a win (a tied match decided by a Super Over counts as a win for the winner) and 1 for each team in a match with no result. The conditions separate teams on equal points by wins, then net run rate, then wickets per ball. We order by points then wins and do not show net run rate, so teams level on both are in alphabetical order; check an official table for the exact order of tied teams. Some matches were decided by the Duckworth-Lewis method after rain.

Season in numbers

The 2018 season had 60 matches (56 in the league stage and 4 in the play-offs), with 19,901 runs scored in all: an average of 331.7 per match for the two teams together, or about 165.8 per team innings. The highest innings was 245 by Kolkata Knight Riders against Kings XI Punjab. There were 872 sixes and 1651 fours, which works out at 14.5 sixes a match. Counting only completed matches, the side batting first won 28 times and the side chasing won 32 times.
