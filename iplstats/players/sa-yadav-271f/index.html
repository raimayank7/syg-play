Career summary

SA Yadav has appeared in the IPL in 14 seasons between 2012 and 2026, playing for Mumbai Indians, Kolkata Knight Riders. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SA Yadav made 4,581 runs in 164 innings, with a batting average of 33.68 and a strike rate of 148.59. That includes 31 fifties and 2 hundreds, 482 fours and 178 sixes. The highest score was 103*, and there were 12 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2025, with 717 runs in 16 innings for Mumbai Indians.

Where this record stands

SA Yadav ranks number 17 on the all-time list for runs, number 18 for sixes (178). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
