Career summary

M Vohra has appeared in the IPL in 9 seasons between 2013 and 2023, playing for Punjab Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Rajasthan Royals, Lucknow Super Giants. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, M Vohra made 1,083 runs in 51 innings, with a batting average of 22.10 and a strike rate of 130.64. That includes 3 fifties and 0 hundreds, 104 fours and 43 sixes. The highest score was 95, and there were 3 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2014, with 324 runs in 8 innings for Punjab Kings.

Where this record stands

M Vohra does not appear in the top 100 of the all-time lists for runs, sixes or wickets, but has enough of a record to be listed here. The all-time lists are on the records page, and the team pages show who led each franchise.
