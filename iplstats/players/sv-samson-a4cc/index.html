Career summary

SV Samson has appeared in the IPL in 14 seasons between 2013 and 2026, playing for Rajasthan Royals, Delhi Capitals, Chennai Super Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SV Samson made 5,181 runs in 186 innings, with a batting average of 31.79 and a strike rate of 141.13. That includes 27 fifties and 5 hundreds, 432 fours and 243 sixes. The highest score was 119, and there were 12 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2024, with 531 runs in 15 innings for Rajasthan Royals.

Where this record stands

SV Samson ranks number 9 on the all-time list for runs, number 6 for sixes (243). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team
