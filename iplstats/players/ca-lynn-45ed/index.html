Career summary

CA Lynn has appeared in the IPL in 7 seasons between 2012 and 2021, playing for Kolkata Knight Riders, Deccan Chargers, Mumbai Indians. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, CA Lynn made 1,329 runs in 42 innings, with a batting average of 34.08 and a strike rate of 140.63. That includes 10 fifties and 0 hundreds, 132 fours and 66 sixes. The highest score was 93*, and there were 3 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2018, with 491 runs in 16 innings for Kolkata Knight Riders.
