Career summary

PA Patel has appeared in the IPL in 12 seasons between 2008 and 2019, playing for Mumbai Indians, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Chennai Super Kings, Kochi Tuskers Kerala, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Deccan Chargers. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, PA Patel made 2,848 runs in 136 innings, with a batting average of 22.60 and a strike rate of 120.78. That includes 13 fifties and 0 hundreds, 365 fours and 49 sixes. The highest score was 81, and there were 13 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2017, with 395 runs in 16 innings for Mumbai Indians.

Where this record stands

PA Patel ranks number 34 on the all-time list for runs, number 95 for sixes (49). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Mumbai Indians, PA Patel ranks number 12 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 911 of the team’s 45,163 runs (2.0%). For Royal Challengers Bengaluru, PA Patel ranks number 11 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 731 of the team’s 44,011 runs (1.7%).
