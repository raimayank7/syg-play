Career summary

KC Sangakkara has appeared in the IPL in 6 seasons between 2008 and 2013, playing for Punjab Kings, Deccan Chargers, Sunrisers Hyderabad. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, KC Sangakkara made 1,687 runs in 68 innings, with a batting average of 25.95 and a strike rate of 121.19. That includes 10 fifties and 0 hundreds, 195 fours and 27 sixes. The highest score was 94, and there were 4 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2011, with 358 runs in 13 innings for Deccan Chargers.

Where this record stands
