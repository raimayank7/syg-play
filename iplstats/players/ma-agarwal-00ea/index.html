Career summary

MA Agarwal has appeared in the IPL in 15 seasons between 2011 and 2025, playing for Punjab Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Rising Pune Supergiant. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, MA Agarwal made 2,756 runs in 125 innings, with a batting average of 22.97 and a strike rate of 133.53. That includes 13 fifties and 1 hundreds, 273 fours and 100 sixes. The highest score was 106, and there were 7 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2021, with 441 runs in 12 innings for Punjab Kings.

Where this record stands

MA Agarwal ranks number 37 on the all-time list for runs, number 49 for sixes (100). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Punjab Kings, MA Agarwal ranks number 5 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,513 of the team’s 43,247 runs (3.5%). For Royal Challengers Bengaluru, MA Agarwal ranks number 16 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 528 of the team’s 44,011 runs (1.2%).
