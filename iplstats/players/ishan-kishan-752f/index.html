Career summary

Ishan Kishan has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2016 and 2026, playing for Mumbai Indians, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Gujarat Lions. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, Ishan Kishan made 3,600 runs in 127 innings, with a batting average of 30.51 and a strike rate of 143.54. That includes 23 fifties and 1 hundreds, 348 fours and 166 sixes. The highest score was 106*, and there were 11 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2026, with 602 runs in 15 innings for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Where this record stands

Ishan Kishan ranks number 24 on the all-time list for runs, number 21 for sixes (166). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Mumbai Indians, Ishan Kishan ranks number 6 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,325 of the team’s 45,163 runs (5.1%). For Sunrisers Hyderabad, Ishan Kishan ranks number 9 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 956 of the team’s 32,885 runs (2.9%).
