Career summary

SPD Smith has appeared in the IPL in 9 seasons between 2012 and 2021, playing for Rajasthan Royals, Pune Warriors, Rising Pune Supergiant, Delhi Capitals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SPD Smith made 2,485 runs in 93 innings, with a batting average of 34.51 and a strike rate of 128.09. That includes 11 fifties and 1 hundreds, 225 fours and 60 sixes. The highest score was 101, and there were 1 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2017, with 472 runs in 15 innings for Rising Pune Supergiant.

Where this record stands

SPD Smith ranks number 45 on the all-time list for runs, number 85 for sixes (60). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
