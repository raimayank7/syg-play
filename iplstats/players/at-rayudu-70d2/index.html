Career summary

AT Rayudu has appeared in the IPL in 14 seasons between 2010 and 2023, playing for Mumbai Indians, Chennai Super Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, AT Rayudu made 4,348 runs in 187 innings, with a batting average of 28.23 and a strike rate of 127.54. That includes 22 fifties and 1 hundreds, 359 fours and 173 sixes. The highest score was 100*, and there were 14 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2018, with 602 runs in 16 innings for Chennai Super Kings.

Where this record stands

AT Rayudu ranks number 18 on the all-time list for runs, number 20 for sixes (173). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Mumbai Indians, AT Rayudu ranks number 4 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,416 of the team’s 45,163 runs (5.3%). For Chennai Super Kings, AT Rayudu ranks number 6 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,932 of the team’s 41,435 runs (4.7%).
