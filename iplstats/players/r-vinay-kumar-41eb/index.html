Career summary

R Vinay Kumar has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2008 and 2018, playing for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Mumbai Indians, Kochi Tuskers Kerala, Kolkata Knight Riders. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, R Vinay Kumar made 310 runs in 43 innings, with a batting average of 11.07 and a strike rate of 113.14. That includes 0 fifties and 0 hundreds, 21 fours and 9 sixes. The highest score was 26*, and there were 4 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2013, with 70 runs in 5 innings for Royal Challengers Bengaluru.

As a bowler, R Vinay Kumar took 105 wickets in 104 innings at an average of 28.25 and an economy rate of 8.39 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 40, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 23, in 2013.
