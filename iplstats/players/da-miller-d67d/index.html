Career summary

DA Miller has appeared in the IPL in 15 seasons between 2012 and 2026, playing for Punjab Kings, Gujarat Titans, Lucknow Super Giants, Delhi Capitals, Rajasthan Royals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, DA Miller made 3,288 runs in 144 innings, with a batting average of 35.74 and a strike rate of 139.50. That includes 14 fifties and 1 hundreds, 235 fours and 152 sixes. The highest score was 101*, and there were 5 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2022, with 481 runs in 16 innings for Gujarat Titans.

Where this record stands

DA Miller ranks number 28 on the all-time list for runs, number 27 for sixes (152). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
