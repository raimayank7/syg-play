Career summary

RA Jadeja has appeared in the IPL in 18 seasons between 2008 and 2026, playing for Chennai Super Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Gujarat Lions, Kochi Tuskers Kerala. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, RA Jadeja made 3,526 runs in 205 innings, with a batting average of 29.14 and a strike rate of 130.64. That includes 5 fifties and 0 hundreds, 264 fours and 122 sixes. The highest score was 77*, and there were 9 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2025, with 301 runs in 14 innings for Chennai Super Kings.

As a bowler, RA Jadeja took 180 wickets in 237 innings at an average of 30.36 and an economy rate of 7.71 runs an over. The best figures were 5 for 16, and there were 4 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 20, in 2023.

RA Jadeja ranks number 25 on the all-time list for runs, number 36 for sixes (122), and number 8 for wickets. The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
