Career summary

Abhishek Sharma has appeared in the IPL in 9 seasons between 2018 and 2026, playing for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Delhi Capitals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, Abhishek Sharma made 2,379 runs in 89 innings, with a batting average of 29.37 and a strike rate of 171.27. That includes 13 fifties and 2 hundreds, 224 fours and 144 sixes. The highest score was 141, and there were 6 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2026, with 563 runs in 15 innings for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

The most wickets in a season were 4, in 2021.
