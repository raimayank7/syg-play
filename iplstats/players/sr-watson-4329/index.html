Career summary

SR Watson has appeared in the IPL in 12 seasons between 2008 and 2020, playing for Rajasthan Royals, Chennai Super Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SR Watson made 3,874 runs in 141 innings, with a batting average of 30.99 and a strike rate of 137.91. That includes 21 fifties and 4 hundreds, 376 fours and 190 sixes. The highest score was 117*, and there were 7 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2018, with 555 runs in 15 innings for Chennai Super Kings.

As a bowler, SR Watson took 92 wickets in 105 innings at an average of 29.15 and an economy rate of 7.93 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 29, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 20, in 2016.

Where this record stands

SR Watson ranks number 22 on the all-time list for runs, number 13 for sixes (190), and number 37 for wickets. The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Rajasthan Royals, SR Watson ranks number 5 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,372 of the team’s 38,488 runs (6.2%). Rajasthan Royals ranks number 4 among the franchise’s bowlers with 61 wickets.
