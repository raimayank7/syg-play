Career summary

Q de Kock has appeared in the IPL in 13 seasons between 2013 and 2026, playing for Mumbai Indians, Lucknow Super Giants, Delhi Capitals, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Kolkata Knight Riders, Sunrisers Hyderabad. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, Q de Kock made 3,441 runs in 118 innings, with a batting average of 31.28 and a strike rate of 134.94. That includes 24 fifties and 3 hundreds, 334 fours and 143 sixes. The highest score was 140*, and there were 4 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2019, with 529 runs in 16 innings for Mumbai Indians.

Where this record stands

Q de Kock ranks number 26 on the all-time list for runs, number 32 for sixes (143). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
