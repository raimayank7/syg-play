Career summary

DA Warner has appeared in the IPL in 15 seasons between 2009 and 2024, playing for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Delhi Capitals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, DA Warner made 6,565 runs in 184 innings, with a batting average of 40.52 and a strike rate of 139.77. That includes 62 fifties and 4 hundreds, 663 fours and 236 sixes. The highest score was 126, and there were 11 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2016, with 848 runs in 17 innings for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Where this record stands

DA Warner ranks number 4 on the all-time list for runs, number 8 for sixes (236). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Sunrisers Hyderabad, DA Warner ranks number 1 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 4,014 of the team’s 32,885 runs (12.2%). For Delhi Capitals, DA Warner ranks number 2 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,551 of the team’s 42,032 runs (6.1%).
