Career summary

SN Thakur has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2015 and 2026, playing for Chennai Super Kings, Delhi Capitals, Kolkata Knight Riders, Rising Pune Supergiant, Lucknow Super Giants, Mumbai Indians, Punjab Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SN Thakur made 349 runs in 44 innings, with a batting average of 11.63 and a strike rate of 132.70. That includes 1 fifties and 0 hundreds, 32 fours and 13 sixes. The highest score was 68, and there were 8 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2022, with 120 runs in 10 innings for Delhi Capitals.

As a bowler, SN Thakur took 119 wickets in 109 innings at an average of 29.53 and an economy rate of 9.57 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 34, and there were 3 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 21, in 2021.
