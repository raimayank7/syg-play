Career summary

CH Morris has appeared in the IPL in 8 seasons between 2013 and 2021, playing for Delhi Capitals, Rajasthan Royals, Chennai Super Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, CH Morris made 618 runs in 49 innings, with a batting average of 22.07 and a strike rate of 155.28. That includes 2 fifties and 0 hundreds, 41 fours and 35 sixes. The highest score was 82*, and there were 9 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2016, with 195 runs in 7 innings for Delhi Capitals.

As a bowler, CH Morris took 95 wickets in 81 innings at an average of 24.16 and an economy rate of 8.01 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 23, and there were 4 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 15, in 2013.
