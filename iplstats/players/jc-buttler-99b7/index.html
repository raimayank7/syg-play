Career summary

JC Buttler has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2016 and 2026, playing for Rajasthan Royals, Gujarat Titans, Mumbai Indians. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, JC Buttler made 4,646 runs in 136 innings, with a batting average of 39.71 and a strike rate of 149.73. That includes 28 fifties and 7 hundreds, 458 fours and 211 sixes. The highest score was 124, and there were 8 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2022, with 863 runs in 17 innings for Rajasthan Royals.

Where this record stands

JC Buttler ranks number 15 on the all-time list for runs, number 11 for sixes (211). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Rajasthan Royals, JC Buttler ranks number 2 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 3,055 of the team’s 38,488 runs (7.9%). For Gujarat Titans, JC Buttler ranks number 3 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,064 of the team’s 13,058 runs (8.1%).
