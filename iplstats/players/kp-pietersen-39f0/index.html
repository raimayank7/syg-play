Career summary

KP Pietersen has appeared in the IPL in 5 seasons between 2009 and 2016, playing for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Rising Pune Supergiant. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, KP Pietersen made 1,001 runs in 36 innings, with a batting average of 37.07 and a strike rate of 134.72. That includes 4 fifties and 1 hundreds, 91 fours and 40 sixes. The highest score was 103*, and there were 3 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2012, with 305 runs in 8 innings for Delhi Capitals.

Where this record stands

KP Pietersen does not appear in the top 100 of the all-time lists for runs, sixes or wickets, but has enough of a record to be listed here. The all-time lists are on the records page, and the team pages show who led each franchise.

In the team

For Royal Challengers Bengaluru, KP Pietersen ranks number 27 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 329 of the team’s 44,011 runs (0.7%). For Delhi Capitals, KP Pietersen ranks number 19 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 599 of the team’s 42,032 runs (1.4%).
