Career summary

AM Rahane has appeared in the IPL in 18 seasons between 2008 and 2026, playing for Rajasthan Royals, Kolkata Knight Riders, Rising Pune Supergiant, Chennai Super Kings, Mumbai Indians, Delhi Capitals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, AM Rahane made 5,367 runs in 197 innings, with a batting average of 30.15 and a strike rate of 125.60. That includes 35 fifties and 2 hundreds, 539 fours and 141 sixes. The highest score was 105*, and there were 15 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2012, with 560 runs in 16 innings for Rajasthan Royals.

Where this record stands

AM Rahane ranks number 8 on the all-time list for runs, number 33 for sixes (141). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Rajasthan Royals, AM Rahane ranks number 3 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,810 of the team’s 38,488 runs (7.3%). For Kolkata Knight Riders, AM Rahane ranks number 19 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 858 of the team’s 41,212 runs (2.1%).
