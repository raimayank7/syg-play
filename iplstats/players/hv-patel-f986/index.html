Career summary

HV Patel has appeared in the IPL in 14 seasons between 2012 and 2026, playing for Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Punjab Kings, Delhi Capitals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, HV Patel made 280 runs in 45 innings, with a batting average of 8.75 and a strike rate of 117.65. That includes 0 fifties and 0 hundreds, 18 fours and 15 sixes. The highest score was 36*, and there were 10 ducks.

The most wickets in a season were 32, in 2021.
