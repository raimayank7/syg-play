Career summary

KH Pandya has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2016 and 2026, playing for Mumbai Indians, Lucknow Super Giants, Royal Challengers Bengaluru. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, KH Pandya made 1,982 runs in 123 innings, with a batting average of 23.32 and a strike rate of 133.83. That includes 3 fifties and 0 hundreds, 173 fours and 76 sixes. The highest score was 86, and there were 7 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2017, with 243 runs in 11 innings for Mumbai Indians.

As a bowler, KH Pandya took 107 wickets in 147 innings at an average of 31.87 and an economy rate of 7.57 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 45, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 17, in 2025.
