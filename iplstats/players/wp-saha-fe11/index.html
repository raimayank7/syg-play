Career summary

WP Saha has appeared in the IPL in 17 seasons between 2008 and 2024, playing for Punjab Kings, Gujarat Titans, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Kolkata Knight Riders, Chennai Super Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, WP Saha made 2,934 runs in 143 innings, with a batting average of 24.25 and a strike rate of 127.57. That includes 13 fifties and 1 hundreds, 296 fours and 87 sixes. The highest score was 115*, and there were 7 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2023, with 371 runs in 17 innings for Gujarat Titans.

Where this record stands

WP Saha ranks number 32 on the all-time list for runs, number 56 for sixes (87). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Punjab Kings, WP Saha ranks number 8 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,115 of the team’s 43,247 runs (2.6%). For Gujarat Titans, WP Saha ranks number 6 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 824 of the team’s 13,058 runs (6.3%).
