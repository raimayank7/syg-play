Career summary

RV Uthappa has appeared in the IPL in 15 seasons between 2008 and 2022, playing for Kolkata Knight Riders, Pune Warriors, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Chennai Super Kings, Mumbai Indians, Rajasthan Royals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, RV Uthappa made 4,952 runs in 197 innings, with a batting average of 27.51 and a strike rate of 130.35. That includes 27 fifties and 0 hundreds, 481 fours and 182 sixes. The highest score was 88, and there were 8 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2014, with 660 runs in 16 innings for Kolkata Knight Riders.

Where this record stands

RV Uthappa ranks number 12 on the all-time list for runs, number 16 for sixes (182). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Kolkata Knight Riders, RV Uthappa ranks number 3 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,439 of the team’s 41,212 runs (5.9%). For Pune Warriors, RV Uthappa ranks number 1 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,103 of the team’s 6,040 runs (18.3%).
