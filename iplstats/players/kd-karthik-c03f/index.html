Career summary

KD Karthik has appeared in the IPL in 17 seasons between 2008 and 2024, playing for Kolkata Knight Riders, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Mumbai Indians, Gujarat Lions, Punjab Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, KD Karthik made 4,842 runs in 233 innings, with a batting average of 26.32 and a strike rate of 135.36. That includes 22 fifties and 0 hundreds, 466 fours and 161 sixes. The highest score was 97*, and there were 18 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2013, with 510 runs in 19 innings for Mumbai Indians.

Where this record stands

KD Karthik ranks number 13 on the all-time list for runs, number 22 for sixes (161). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Kolkata Knight Riders, KD Karthik ranks number 13 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,143 of the team’s 41,212 runs (2.8%). For Royal Challengers Bengaluru, KD Karthik ranks number 9 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 937 of the team’s 44,011 runs (2.1%).
