Career summary

KL Rahul has appeared in the IPL in 13 seasons between 2013 and 2026, playing for Punjab Kings, Lucknow Super Giants, Delhi Capitals, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Royal Challengers Bengaluru. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, KL Rahul made 5,815 runs in 150 innings, with a batting average of 46.15 and a strike rate of 139.15. That includes 45 fifties and 6 hundreds, 508 fours and 239 sixes. The highest score was 152*, and there were 5 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2020, with 670 runs in 14 innings for Punjab Kings.

Where this record stands

KL Rahul ranks number 5 on the all-time list for runs, number 7 for sixes (239). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Punjab Kings, KL Rahul ranks number 1 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,548 of the team’s 43,247 runs (5.9%). For Lucknow Super Giants, KL Rahul ranks number 2 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,410 of the team’s 12,062 runs (11.7%).
