Career summary

HH Pandya has appeared in the IPL in 12 seasons between 2015 and 2026, playing for Mumbai Indians, Gujarat Titans. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, HH Pandya made 2,955 runs in 150 innings, with a batting average of 27.88 and a strike rate of 146.29. That includes 10 fifties and 0 hundreds, 228 fours and 156 sixes. The highest score was 91, and there were 6 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2022, with 487 runs in 15 innings for Gujarat Titans.

As a bowler, HH Pandya took 82 wickets in 116 innings at an average of 33.55 and an economy rate of 9.36 runs an over. The best figures were 5 for 36, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 18, in 2018.
