Career summary

MK Pandey has appeared in the IPL in 19 seasons between 2008 and 2026, playing for Kolkata Knight Riders, Sunrisers Hyderabad, Pune Warriors, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Lucknow Super Giants, Mumbai Indians. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, MK Pandey made 4,012 runs in 163 innings, with a batting average of 29.50 and a strike rate of 121.83. That includes 22 fifties and 1 hundreds, 348 fours and 117 sixes. The highest score was 114*, and there were 14 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2020, with 425 runs in 15 innings for Sunrisers Hyderabad.

Where this record stands

MK Pandey ranks number 21 on the all-time list for runs, number 40 for sixes (117). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Kolkata Knight Riders, MK Pandey ranks number 7 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,474 of the team’s 41,212 runs (3.6%). For Sunrisers Hyderabad, MK Pandey ranks number 7 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,345 of the team’s 32,885 runs (4.1%).
