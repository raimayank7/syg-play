Career summary

MP Stoinis has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2016 and 2026, playing for Punjab Kings, Lucknow Super Giants, Delhi Capitals, Royal Challengers Bengaluru. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, MP Stoinis made 2,242 runs in 108 innings, with a batting average of 28.74 and a strike rate of 147.50. That includes 10 fifties and 1 hundreds, 181 fours and 117 sixes. The highest score was 124*, and there were 10 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2023, with 408 runs in 15 innings for Lucknow Super Giants.

As a bowler, MP Stoinis took 46 wickets in 75 innings at an average of 33.63 and an economy rate of 9.93 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 15, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 13, in 2020.

Where this record stands

MP Stoinis ranks number 53 on the all-time list for runs, number 39 for sixes (117), and number 85 for wickets. The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
