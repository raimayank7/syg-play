Career summary

S Dhawan has appeared in the IPL in 17 seasons between 2008 and 2024, playing for Sunrisers Hyderabad, Delhi Capitals, Deccan Chargers, Punjab Kings, Mumbai Indians. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, S Dhawan made 6,769 runs in 221 innings, with a batting average of 35.26 and a strike rate of 127.14. That includes 51 fifties and 2 hundreds, 768 fours and 153 sixes. The highest score was 106*, and there were 11 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2020, with 618 runs in 17 innings for Delhi Capitals.

Where this record stands

S Dhawan ranks number 3 on the all-time list for runs, number 26 for sixes (153). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Sunrisers Hyderabad, S Dhawan ranks number 2 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,518 of the team’s 32,885 runs (7.7%). For Delhi Capitals, S Dhawan ranks number 5 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,066 of the team’s 42,032 runs (4.9%).
