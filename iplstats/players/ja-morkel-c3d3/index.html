Career summary

JA Morkel has appeared in the IPL in 9 seasons between 2008 and 2016, playing for Chennai Super Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Rising Pune Supergiant. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, JA Morkel made 974 runs in 67 innings, with a batting average of 24.35 and a strike rate of 141.98. That includes 3 fifties and 0 hundreds, 61 fours and 55 sixes. The highest score was 73*, and there were 3 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2008, with 241 runs in 10 innings for Chennai Super Kings.

As a bowler, JA Morkel took 85 wickets in 87 innings at an average of 27.75 and an economy rate of 8.21 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 32, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 17, in 2008.
