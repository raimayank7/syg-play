Career summary

V Sehwag has appeared in the IPL in 8 seasons between 2008 and 2015, playing for Delhi Capitals, Punjab Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, V Sehwag made 2,728 runs in 104 innings, with a batting average of 27.56 and a strike rate of 155.44. That includes 16 fifties and 2 hundreds, 334 fours and 106 sixes. The highest score was 122, and there were 7 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2012, with 495 runs in 16 innings for Delhi Capitals.

Where this record stands

V Sehwag ranks number 39 on the all-time list for runs, number 47 for sixes (106). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Delhi Capitals, V Sehwag ranks number 4 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 2,174 of the team’s 42,032 runs (5.2%). For Punjab Kings, V Sehwag ranks number 29 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 554 of the team’s 43,247 runs (1.3%).
