Career summary

MK Tiwary has appeared in the IPL in 10 seasons between 2008 and 2018, playing for Kolkata Knight Riders, Delhi Capitals, Rising Pune Supergiant, Punjab Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, MK Tiwary made 1,695 runs in 83 innings, with a batting average of 28.73 and a strike rate of 116.98. That includes 7 fifties and 0 hundreds, 156 fours and 40 sixes. The highest score was 75*, and there were 8 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2011, with 359 runs in 13 innings for Kolkata Knight Riders.
