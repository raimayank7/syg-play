Career summary

SL Malinga has appeared in the IPL in 9 seasons between 2009 and 2019, playing for Mumbai Indians. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SL Malinga made 88 runs in 22 innings, with a batting average of 5.50 and a strike rate of 88.89. That includes 0 fifties and 0 hundreds, 6 fours and 5 sixes. The highest score was 17, and there were 6 ducks.
