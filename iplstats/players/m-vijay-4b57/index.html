Career summary

M Vijay has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2009 and 2020, playing for Chennai Super Kings, Punjab Kings, Delhi Capitals. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, M Vijay made 2,619 runs in 106 innings, with a batting average of 25.93 and a strike rate of 121.87. That includes 13 fifties and 2 hundreds, 247 fours and 91 sixes. The highest score was 127, and there were 5 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2010, with 458 runs in 15 innings for Chennai Super Kings.

Where this record stands

M Vijay ranks number 41 on the all-time list for runs, number 55 for sixes (91). The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.

In the team

For Chennai Super Kings, M Vijay ranks number 9 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 1,708 of the team’s 41,435 runs (4.1%). For Punjab Kings, M Vijay ranks number 21 among all the franchise’s players by runs: 704 of the team’s 43,247 runs (1.6%).
