Career summary

Harbhajan Singh has appeared in the IPL in 13 seasons between 2008 and 2021, playing for Mumbai Indians, Chennai Super Kings, Kolkata Knight Riders. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, Harbhajan Singh made 833 runs in 89 innings, with a batting average of 15.72 and a strike rate of 137.91. That includes 1 fifties and 0 hundreds, 79 fours and 42 sixes. The highest score was 64, and there were 13 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2015, with 117 runs in 9 innings for Mumbai Indians.

As a bowler, Harbhajan Singh took 150 wickets in 160 innings at an average of 26.87 and an economy rate of 7.08 runs an over. The best figures were 5 for 18, and there were 2 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 24, in 2013.
