Career summary

SP Narine has appeared in the IPL in 15 seasons between 2012 and 2026, playing for Kolkata Knight Riders. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SP Narine made 1,820 runs in 124 innings, with a batting average of 17.50 and a strike rate of 165.30. That includes 7 fifties and 1 hundreds, 191 fours and 120 sixes. The highest score was 109, and there were 18 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2024, with 488 runs in 14 innings for Kolkata Knight Riders.

As a bowler, SP Narine took 207 wickets in 200 innings at an average of 25.42 and an economy rate of 6.79 runs an over. The best figures were 5 for 19, and there were 8 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 24, in 2012.

Where this record stands

SP Narine ranks number 69 on the all-time list for runs, number 37 for sixes (120), and number 3 for wickets. The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
