Career summary

GJ Maxwell has appeared in the IPL in 13 seasons between 2012 and 2025, playing for Punjab Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru, Delhi Capitals, Mumbai Indians. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, GJ Maxwell made 2,819 runs in 134 innings, with a batting average of 23.89 and a strike rate of 155.15. That includes 18 fifties and 0 hundreds, 237 fours and 161 sixes. The highest score was 95, and there were 19 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2014, with 552 runs in 16 innings for Punjab Kings.

As a bowler, GJ Maxwell took 41 wickets in 85 innings at an average of 34.46 and an economy rate of 8.30 runs an over. The best figures were 2 for 15, and there were 0 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 7, in 2017.
