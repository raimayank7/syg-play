Career summary

DJ Bravo has appeared in the IPL in 14 seasons between 2008 and 2022, playing for Chennai Super Kings, Mumbai Indians, Gujarat Lions. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, DJ Bravo made 1,560 runs in 110 innings, with a batting average of 22.61 and a strike rate of 129.57. That includes 5 fifties and 0 hundreds, 120 fours and 66 sixes. The highest score was 70*, and there were 5 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2012, with 371 runs in 16 innings for Chennai Super Kings.

As a bowler, DJ Bravo took 183 wickets in 158 innings at an average of 23.83 and an economy rate of 8.38 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 22, and there were 2 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 32, in 2013.

Where this record stands

DJ Bravo ranks number 79 on the all-time list for runs, number 75 for sixes (66), and number 7 for wickets. The lists count 964 players in all, so these places are among the leading names of the league; see the all-time records to compare.
