Career summary

R Ashwin has appeared in the IPL in 16 seasons between 2009 and 2025, playing for Chennai Super Kings, Rajasthan Royals, Punjab Kings, Delhi Capitals, Rising Pune Supergiant. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, R Ashwin made 833 runs in 94 innings, with a batting average of 13.02 and a strike rate of 118.16. That includes 1 fifties and 0 hundreds, 64 fours and 29 sixes. The highest score was 50, and there were 12 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2022, with 191 runs in 12 innings for Rajasthan Royals.

As a bowler, R Ashwin took 187 wickets in 217 innings at an average of 30.22 and an economy rate of 7.20 runs an over. The best figures were 4 for 34, and there were 1 innings with four or more wickets.

The most wickets in a season were 20, in 2011.
