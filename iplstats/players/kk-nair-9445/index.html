Career summary

KK Nair has appeared in the IPL in 11 seasons between 2013 and 2026, playing for Delhi Capitals, Rajasthan Royals, Punjab Kings, Royal Challengers Bengaluru. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, KK Nair made 1,712 runs in 78 innings, with a batting average of 23.45 and a strike rate of 131.29. That includes 11 fifties and 0 hundreds, 188 fours and 49 sixes. The highest score was 89, and there were 5 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2016, with 357 runs in 12 innings for Delhi Capitals.

Where this record stands
