Career summary

SE Marsh has appeared in the IPL in 9 seasons between 2008 and 2017, playing for Punjab Kings. All figures below come from ball-by-ball data and count league and play-off matches but not super overs.

As a batter, SE Marsh made 2,477 runs in 69 innings, with a batting average of 39.95 and a strike rate of 132.74. That includes 20 fifties and 1 hundreds, 266 fours and 78 sixes. The highest score was 115, and there were 1 ducks.

The best season with the bat was 2008, with 616 runs in 11 innings for Punjab Kings.
