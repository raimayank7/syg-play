Style of play

Chennai Super Kings won the toss in 133 matches and chose to field in 72 of them (54%). Batting first it won 74 of 144 decided matches (51%); chasing it won 74 of 121 (61%). The team reached the play-offs in 12 of its 17 seasons. Its best league record by win share was in 2021 (69% of decided matches) and its weakest was in 2022 (29%). The player most often named player of the match for the team is MS Dhoni (16 times). Its most used ground was MA Chidambaram Stadium with 84 matches.

Totals

The highest total was 246 against Rajasthan Royals (2010-04-03), and the lowest all-out total was 79 against Mumbai Indians (2013-05-05). Over all innings the team scored at 8.47 runs an over. Its win rate of 55.8% is worked out as wins divided by all matches with a result.
