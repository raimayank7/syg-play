Style of play

Sunrisers Hyderabad won the toss in 100 matches and chose to field in 61 of them (61%). Batting first it won 53 of 112 decided matches (47%); chasing it won 50 of 98 (51%). The team reached the play-offs in 8 of its 14 seasons. Its best league record by win share was in 2016 (65% of decided matches) and its weakest was in 2021 (21%).
