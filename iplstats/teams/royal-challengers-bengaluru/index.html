Style of play

Royal Challengers Bengaluru won the toss in 136 matches and chose to field in 101 of them (74%). Batting first it won 67 of 143 decided matches (47%); chasing it won 78 of 140 (56%). The team reached the play-offs in 11 of its 19 seasons. Its best league record by win share was in 2025 (73% of decided matches) and its weakest was in 2017 (23%). The player most often named player of the match for the team is AB de Villiers (23 times). Its most used ground was M Chinnaswamy Stadium, Bengaluru with 100 matches.

Totals

The highest total was 263 against Pune Warriors (2013-04-23), and the lowest all-out total was 49 against Kolkata Knight Riders (2017-04-23). Over all innings the team scored at 8.59 runs an over. Its win rate of 51.2% is worked out as wins divided by all matches with a result.
