Style of play

Kolkata Knight Riders won the toss in 135 matches and chose to field in 93 of them (69%). Batting first it won 58 of 132 decided matches (44%); chasing it won 84 of 144 (58%). The team reached the play-offs in 8 of its 19 seasons. Its best league record by win share was in 2024 (79% of decided matches) and its weakest was in 2009 (23%). The player most often named player of the match for the team is SP Narine (18 times). Its most used ground was Eden Gardens, Kolkata with 102 matches.

Totals

The highest total was 272 against Delhi Capitals (2024-04-03), and the lowest all-out total was 67 against Mumbai Indians (2008-05-16). Over all innings the team scored at 8.39 runs an over. Its win rate of 51.4% is worked out as wins divided by all matches with a result.
