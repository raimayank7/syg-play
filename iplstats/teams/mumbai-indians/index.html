Style of play

Mumbai Indians won the toss in 160 matches and chose to field in 104 of them (65%). Batting first it won 78 of 150 decided matches (52%); chasing it won 79 of 141 (56%). The team reached the play-offs in 11 of its 19 seasons. Its best league record by win share was in 2017 (71% of decided matches) and its weakest was in 2022 (29%). The player most often named player of the match for the team is RG Sharma (19 times). Its most used ground was Wankhede Stadium, Mumbai with 99 matches.

Totals

The highest total was 247 against Delhi Capitals (2024-04-27), and the lowest all-out total was 87 against Punjab Kings (2011-05-10). Over all innings the team scored at 8.49 runs an over. Its win rate of 54.0% is worked out as wins divided by all matches with a result.
