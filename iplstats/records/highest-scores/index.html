About this list

An asterisk after a score means the batter was not out. The list uses the best score of each batter in each season, because that is what our compact data keeps. Look at the Season column to see whether a record came early or late in the history of the league. High scores depend on the day: the pitch, the boundary size and the opposition bowling all play a part.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 25.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
