About this list

Wickets are the bowler’s main currency, but the economy column matters too, because in a short format a wicket that costs many runs may not help the team. In the data, 31 bowlers have taken 100 or more wickets and 14 have taken 150 or more. Averages here are runs conceded per wicket, so lower is better. Spinners and fast bowlers appear side by side; the data does not record bowling style, so we do not label them.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 30.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
