About this list

A big season needs fitness and form together, plus a team that goes deep in the play-offs, since extra matches give extra innings. The list shows each batter’s total for one season and team, so a batter who moved teams in the middle of a season appears once for each team. The seasons themselves are not equal in length: the league has had between 8 and 10 teams and between 59 and 74 matches a year, which affects how many innings are possible.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 30.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
