About this list

The award is chosen by the match officials or a panel, and the data records the name. It is a good sign of impact, since the award usually goes to the player who did most to decide the match. The list has 321 different winners in all, out of 964 players in the data, so a small group wins a large share.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 30.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
