About this list

Fours are the bread and butter of an innings. Players near the top of this list tend to be openers and top-order batters who face many balls, so the count is partly a measure of opportunity. A batter’s runs from boundaries can be estimated as fours times four plus sixes times six; compare that with total runs to see how much of the score came from singles, twos and threes.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 30.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
