About this list

A team total is the runs made in one innings, with wickets lost shown after the slash. The longer the innings, the more runs, so compare totals in full 20-over innings; an innings shortened by rain or by a chase ending early is not comparable. Notice how many of the highest totals come from recent seasons: scoring rates have risen over the history of the league.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 25.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
