About this list

Career run totals reward both quality and longevity: a batter needs to score well for many seasons, and to keep their place in a team. In the data, 30 players have scored 3,000 or more IPL runs and 10 have passed 5,000. Look at the average and strike rate columns beside the runs to see how each batter got there: a high strike rate with a modest average describes an attacking player, a high average with a lower strike rate an anchor. Click a name for the season-by-season story.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 30.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
