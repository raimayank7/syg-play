About this list

Only innings in which the team was bowled out are listed, since a team that was chasing and stopped when the match ended has not really been dismissed cheaply. The overs column shows how long the innings lasted; a low total in few overs usually means a collapse. For the other end of the scale see the highest team totals list.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 25.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
