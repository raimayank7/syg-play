About this list

Sixes have become the signature of the format. The balls-per-six column shows how often a batter clears the rope: a smaller number means a six more frequently. Batters who play many seasons collect large totals, so compare this column as well as the count. Sixes are counted only when the ball was hit off the bat for six runs; overthrows and boundaries by extras are not included.

How the figures are counted

All numbers come from Cricsheet ball-by-ball data for 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026. Super overs are not included. For the exact rules, read the methods page. Lists show the top 30.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
