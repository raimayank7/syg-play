Questions people ask

Where does the data come from?

From Cricsheet’s ball-by-ball files, released under the Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0. We calculate all figures from them.

Is this the official IPL site?

No. It is an independent reference and is not connected with the BCCI, the league or any team. There are no logos or photographs.

Are the figures up to date?

They cover the matches in the data we downloaded on 13 November 2026, up to the 2026 season. We update the site from time to time.

Why does a figure differ from another site?

Usually because of counting rules for super overs, retired batters and rain-affected matches. Our rules are on the methods page.
