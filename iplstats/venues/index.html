How to use this table

A high average first-innings score suggests a batting-friendly pitch or short boundaries. A bat-first win share well below 50% means the side chasing has had the better of the matches at that ground, often because of dew in the evening or because captains prefer to chase. Names of grounds that were renamed are combined: for example the Delhi ground is counted under Arun Jaitley Stadium, and the Ahmedabad ground under Narendra Modi Stadium. Matches played in South Africa in 2009 and in the United Arab Emirates are included where a ground reached twenty matches.

Data: Cricsheet, ODC-By 1.0. Independent site, not connected with the BCCI or the league.
