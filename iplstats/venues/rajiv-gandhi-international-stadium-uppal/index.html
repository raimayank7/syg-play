Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal: IPL record

90 matches from 2008 to 2026. Average first-innings score 168.3; teams batting first won 45% of decided matches.

What the numbers say

In 90 IPL matches at Rajiv Gandhi International Stadium, Uppal the first innings averaged 168.3 runs. The side batting first won 40 of the 89 matches that had a result (45%), so the chasing side won the other 49. The highest total was 286 by Sunrisers Hyderabad on 2025-03-23. Seasons with matches here: 2008, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026.

The venue name is written as in the data.

Toss, scoring and boundaries

The captain winning the toss chose to field first in 52 of 90 matches (58%). Teams chasing won 49 of 89 decided matches here (55%). Across the matches both teams hit 1115 sixes and 2495 fours: 12.4 sixes and 27.7 fours per match, which tells you how short or long the boundaries play. The highest total successfully chased here was 247 by Sunrisers Hyderabad on 2025-04-12, and the lowest total successfully defended was 126 by Sunrisers Hyderabad on 2013-04-05. The lowest all-out total was 80 by Delhi Capitals on 2013-05-04. The teams that have played most often at this ground are Sunrisers Hyderabad (70 matches), Deccan Chargers (18 matches), Punjab Kings (14 matches).
