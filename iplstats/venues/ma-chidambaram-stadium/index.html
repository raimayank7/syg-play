MA Chidambaram Stadium: IPL record

98 matches from 2008 to 2026. Average first-innings score 165.6; teams batting first won 54% of decided matches.

What the numbers say

In 98 IPL matches at MA Chidambaram Stadium the first innings averaged 165.6 runs. The side batting first won 53 of the 98 matches that had a result (54%), so the chasing side won the other 45. The highest total was 246 by Chennai Super Kings on 2010-04-03. Seasons with matches here: 2008, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2015, 2018, 2019, 2021, 2023, 2024, 2025, 2026.

The venue name is written as in the data.

Toss, scoring and boundaries

The captain winning the toss chose to field first in 47 of 98 matches (48%). Teams chasing won 45 of 98 decided matches here (46%). Across the matches both teams hit 1126 sixes and 2533 fours: 11.5 sixes and 25.8 fours per match, which tells you how short or long the boundaries play. The highest total successfully chased here was 213 by Lucknow Super Giants on 2024-04-23, and the lowest total successfully defended was 126 by Royal Challengers Bengaluru on 2008-05-21. The lowest all-out total was 70 by Royal Challengers Bengaluru on 2019-03-23. The teams that have played most often at this ground are Chennai Super Kings (84 matches), Mumbai Indians (17 matches), Kolkata Knight Riders (17 matches).
