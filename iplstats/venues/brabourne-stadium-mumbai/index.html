Brabourne Stadium, Mumbai: IPL record

27 matches from 2010 to 2022. Average first-innings score 178.5; teams batting first won 52% of decided matches.

What the numbers say

In 27 IPL matches at Brabourne Stadium, Mumbai the first innings averaged 178.5 runs. The side batting first won 14 of the 27 matches that had a result (52%), so the chasing side won the other 13. The highest total was 217 by Rajasthan Royals on 2022-04-18. Seasons with matches here: 2010, 2014, 2015, 2022.

The venue name is written as in the data.

Toss, scoring and boundaries

The captain winning the toss chose to field first in 18 of 27 matches (67%). Teams chasing won 13 of 27 decided matches here (48%). Across the matches both teams hit 333 sixes and 874 fours: 12.3 sixes and 32.4 fours per match, which tells you how short or long the boundaries play. The highest total successfully chased here was 211 by Lucknow Super Giants on 2022-03-31, and the lowest total successfully defended was 177 by Mumbai Indians on 2022-05-06. The lowest all-out total was 68 by Royal Challengers Bengaluru on 2022-04-23. The teams that have played most often at this ground are Mumbai Indians (11 matches), Rajasthan Royals (7 matches), Delhi Capitals (6 matches).
