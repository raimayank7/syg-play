Punjab Cricket Association IS Bindra Stadium, Mohali, Chandigarh: IPL record

61 matches from 2008 to 2023. Average first-innings score 168.3; teams batting first won 44% of decided matches.

What the numbers say

In 61 IPL matches at Punjab Cricket Association IS Bindra Stadium, Mohali, Chandigarh the first innings averaged 168.3 runs. The side batting first won 27 of the 61 matches that had a result (44%), so the chasing side won the other 34. The highest total was 257 by Lucknow Super Giants on 2023-04-28. Seasons with matches here: 2008, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2019, 2023.

Matches played under the name Punjab Cricket Association Stadium, Mohali, Chandigarh are counted here too, because it is the same ground.

Toss, scoring and boundaries

The captain winning the toss chose to field first in 44 of 61 matches (72%). Teams chasing won 34 of 61 decided matches here (56%). Across the matches both teams hit 602 sixes and 1839 fours: 9.9 sixes and 30.1 fours per match, which tells you how short or long the boundaries play. The highest total successfully chased here was 216 by Mumbai Indians on 2023-05-03, and the lowest total successfully defended was 106 by Punjab Kings on 2015-05-13. The lowest all-out total was 67 by Delhi Capitals on 2017-04-30. The teams that have played most often at this ground are Punjab Kings (61 matches), Mumbai Indians (9 matches), Kolkata Knight Riders (8 matches).
