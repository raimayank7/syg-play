Maharashtra Cricket Association Stadium, Pune: IPL record

42 matches from 2013 to 2022. Average first-innings score 164.8; teams batting first won 52% of decided matches.

What the numbers say

In 42 IPL matches at Maharashtra Cricket Association Stadium, Pune the first innings averaged 164.8 runs. The side batting first won 22 of the 42 matches that had a result (52%), so the chasing side won the other 20. The highest total was 211 by Chennai Super Kings on 2018-04-30. Seasons with matches here: 2013, 2015, 2016, 2017, 2018, 2022.

The venue name is written as in the data.

Toss, scoring and boundaries

The captain winning the toss chose to field first in 31 of 42 matches (74%). Teams chasing won 20 of 42 decided matches here (48%). Across the matches both teams hit 523 sixes and 1036 fours: 12.5 sixes and 24.7 fours per match, which tells you how short or long the boundaries play. The highest total successfully chased here was 196 by Gujarat Lions on 2016-04-29, and the lowest total successfully defended was 119 by Sunrisers Hyderabad on 2013-04-17. The lowest all-out total was 73 by Punjab Kings on 2017-05-14. The teams that have played most often at this ground are Rising Pune Supergiant (11 matches), Chennai Super Kings (10 matches), Pune Warriors (8 matches).
