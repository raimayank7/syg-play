IPL Stats

How we count IPL statistics

Every number on this site is calculated by us from the ball-by-ball files published by Cricsheet. This page explains the rules, so you can check or reproduce any figure.

The data

The files are the IPL download from cricsheet.org, version 1.3.0 JSON, released under the Open Data Commons Attribution License 1.0, which requires credit to the source: Data: Cricsheet. We downloaded them on 13 November 2026. They hold 1243 matches from 2008 to 2026, one file per match.

Batting

  • Runs are the runs scored off the bat. Extras are not credited to the batter.
  • Balls faced include every ball bowled to the batter except wides.
  • Innings counts every match in which the batter faced a ball or was dismissed.
  • Not out is an innings in which the batter was not dismissed. A batter who retired hurt is counted as not out.
  • Average = runs ÷ (innings − not outs). Strike rate = runs ÷ balls faced × 100.
  • Fours and sixes count only boundaries hit off the bat, not overthrows or byes.

Bowling

  • Wickets are bowled, caught, caught and bowled, lbw, stumped and hit wicket. Run outs, retired batters and obstructing the field are not credited to the bowler.
  • Runs conceded include wides and no-balls but not byes, leg byes or penalty runs.
  • Balls bowled do not include wides or no-balls. Economy = runs ÷ overs. Average = runs ÷ wickets.

Teams and seasons

  • Super overs are left out of all innings, totals and player figures. A match decided by a super over is shown with the winner of the super over.
  • Teams that changed name (Bangalore to Bengaluru, Delhi Daredevils to Delhi Capitals, Kings XI Punjab to Punjab Kings, and the two spellings of Rising Pune Supergiant) are combined.
  • League points follow the IPL Playing Conditions 2026, clause 16.10.1 (effective 1 March 2026, read on iplt20.com on 13 November 2026): 2 for a win, 1 for each team when there is no result, 0 for a loss. Net run rate (clause 16.12: all-out innings count the full quota of overs, only matches with a result count) is not worked out here, so teams level on points and wins may be in a different order from the official table.
  • The season is the year of the first match.

Checks we made

We compared a sample of results with widely known records, such as the highest season total of runs and the wicket totals of the leading bowlers, and the figures matched. Small differences from other sources can arise from how rain-affected matches and substitute players are handled.

For explanations of the statistics themselves, see our guide to reading cricket statistics.
