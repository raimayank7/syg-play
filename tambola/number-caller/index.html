Tambola

Tambola number caller

Press the button for the next number. Called numbers light up on the board, so you can check any claim at once. No number comes twice.

How it works

  • Each press draws one of the numbers not yet called, chosen at random.
  • The board shows all 90 numbers, with the called ones highlighted and the latest outlined.
  • Under the number is a short phrase from our number names.
  • Turn on "Say the number aloud" for a voice (your phone or computer supplies it, and it depends on the voices installed).
  • Undo last puts the number back if you pressed by mistake. New game clears the board.

Your game is saved

The called numbers are kept in your browser, so a refresh does not lose the game. Nothing is sent to us. Use New game to start over.

Calling tips

Say each number twice, once whole and once as digits. A steady pace of one number every five to eight seconds works for most rooms. More in how to be a good caller.

Using it on a screen

On a laptop connected to a projector, open this page and press the large button. Everyone in the room sees the board and the called numbers, so you do not need to read them all out. On a phone, keep the screen awake in your settings, because a locked phone may lose the page.

Checking a claim

Look at the board, which lights every called number. If all of a player’s numbers are lit, the claim is good. The latest number is outlined, so it is easy to see whether a late claim came before or after it.
