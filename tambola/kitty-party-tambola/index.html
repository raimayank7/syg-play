Tambola for kitty parties

At a kitty party, tambola does what no other game does so well: everyone can play, nobody needs to be good at anything and the host does not need much preparation. A few touches make it feel special.

Ticket themes

  • Print the party name on the tickets. In the ticket maker, the name box adds it above the strip.
  • Use a colour for each table so that tickets do not get mixed up.
  • For a theme party, match the prizes to the theme: a Diwali party with diyas, a monsoon party with chai cups.

Quick prizes keep the room lively

Kitty parties are often limited in time. Plan for about 45 minutes and use quick prizes: early five, three lines, four corners and one full house. Call numbers briskly, and leave the second full house for the next kitty.

A caller with a personality

The caller makes or breaks the game. Pick the friend who is not afraid to be funny, and give them our number names to read. Add a rule that the caller may be teased but never rushed.

Keeping it friendly

  • Agree prizes in advance and say them aloud.
  • Rotate hosting, and let the previous winner choose the next prizes.
  • Keep a small "everybody" gift for people who win nothing, so the evening ends well.

Gifts and fees

Many groups collect a small amount for prizes. If you do, say clearly what it will be spent on, and consider whether the rules of your area or society cover games with an entry fee. A safer way is to buy prizes from the host’s own budget or from the pooled kitty, and not tie them to who pays to play. More in prize ideas.

A sample 45-minute plan

MinutesWhat happens
0 to 5Hand out tickets, announce the prizes and the rules.
5 to 15Early five and the three lines.
15 to 30Four corners and the first full house.
30 to 40Second full house, or a quick special round.
40 to 45Winners’ photograph and thanks.

Use the number caller on a phone and let a guest hold it, so the host can mingle. If the game is shorter than planned, a second round with new tickets is easy to start: press Make tickets on the maker and print again.
