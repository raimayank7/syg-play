Tambola number names: 40 to 49

Fun phrases for tambola numbers 40 to 49, one for each number, for callers who want to keep a room awake. Say the number, then the phrase.

  • 40a round number with a smile
  • 41the odd one out
  • 42the answer to a famous joke
  • 43a flag on the hill
  • 44two chairs side by side
  • 45halfway to ninety
  • 46a pair of sixes plus four
  • 47up the stairs we go
  • 48a train waiting at the platform
  • 49one short of fifty

About the 40 to 49 column

The forties are the central column of the ticket, the fifth of nine. Many players watch this column first because it sits in the middle of the ticket. Across a strip of six tickets this column holds all 10 of its numbers, and a single ticket carries between one and three of them, which is 1.7 per ticket on average.

How to use these names

Say the number first, twice, once whole and once digit by digit. Then add the phrase, keeping it shorter than a sentence. The phrases are our own and quite silly on purpose: no group is required to use them, and you may swap in your own jokes, local names and family in-jokes. Callers who add something of their own, such as the host’s name for the number of their birthday, get the biggest laughs.

Numbers that are easy to hear wrongly

Teen numbers and tens numbers end alike when spoken, "teen" and "ty", and players mark the wrong one. Say each of these as a whole number and then as separate digits.

  • 40 sounds like 14: say "4 0" for 40.

A quick game for this column

Offer a "middle column" prize for the first player who completes the fifth column. It rewards a ticket with the central numbers.

Numbers in this group

Of the 10 numbers in this group, 5 are even and 5 are odd, 2 are a multiple of five, and 3 are prime (41, 43, 47). Callers sometimes use these facts as a quick quiz: before calling, ask the room "is it odd or even?", then reveal the number. It slows the game a little and keeps everyone listening.
