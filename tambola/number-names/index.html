Tambola number names

Every number from 1 to 90 has its own short phrase here, for callers who want a livelier game. Choose a group of ten.

  • 1 to 9
  • 10 to 19
  • 20 to 29
  • 30 to 39
  • 40 to 49
  • 50 to 59
  • 60 to 69
  • 70 to 79
  • 80 to 90

Why number names?

Callers in many places add a nickname to each number: a rhyme, a picture, a joke. It slows the game a little, and makes the room laugh. These are our own, so they do not match the traditional calls you may know. Mix them with yours.

Each group page shows the numbers of one ticket column, so you can also use them as a cheat sheet for the board. The number caller shows the same phrase under every number it draws.
