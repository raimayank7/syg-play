How many tambola tickets do you need?

A tambola ticket carries 15 of the 90 numbers. A strip is six tickets that carry all 90 between them, once each. Knowing this tells you how many tickets to print for a group and how many players the game can handle.

Strips are rounded up. Four players with one ticket each use four of the six tickets of a strip.

Why strips matter

If six players hold the six tickets of one strip, every number that is called is on exactly one of those tickets. Nobody is left out of the numbers, and the game always ends on somebody’s full house. If tickets come from different strips, two players may have the same number and can claim at the same time. This is allowed in the game but needs a rule for sharing.

How many tickets per player?

  • One ticket suits beginners and children.
  • Two tickets give more chances and more to watch. Many adults like this.
  • More than three is hard to follow at a normal calling speed. Reserve it for players who want a challenge.

Printing

Each press of the Make tickets button on the ticket maker creates new random strips, and each is checked by the page before it is shown: every ticket has 15 numbers, 5 in each row, at least one in each column, and a strip uses 1 to 90 once. Print on A4 paper and cut the strip into six tickets with scissors. Do not photocopy the same strip for two players unless you want twins.

Sums for the host

For N players with t tickets each you need N × t tickets, which is N × t ÷ 6 strips, rounded up. Spare tickets cost nothing and are useful for latecomers.

See also the odds of tambola and how to host a tambola party.

An example

For a kitty party with 18 guests and two tickets each you need 36 tickets, which is six strips. For a hall with 120 people and one ticket each you need 120 tickets, which is 20 strips. If some guests may arrive late, add a strip.
