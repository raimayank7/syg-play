A game anyone can play

Tambola needs no skill, only luck, a ticket and some patience. That is why it is played at kitty parties, society festivals, weddings and family evenings. This site gives hosts everything in one place: tickets that are valid, a caller that never repeats a number, rules and prize lists you can print, and plenty of ideas.

Questions people ask

What is the difference between tambola and housie?

They are two names for much the same game: numbers 1 to 90, tickets of 15 numbers, prizes for lines and a full house.

Are the tickets valid?

Yes. Each strip is checked before it is shown: six tickets, 15 numbers each, five in every row, every number from 1 to 90 used once.

Do I need to pay or log in?

No. The tickets, caller and bingo cards are free and nothing is stored on our side.

Can I use this for gambling?

We built it for family games and parties and do not take bets. If you plan to play for money, check the rules of your area first.
