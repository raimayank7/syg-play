Word Search

How to play a word search

A word search is a grid of letters with a list of hidden words. Your job is to find every word on the list. The words run in straight lines: across, down or diagonally, and in harder puzzles also backwards. It needs only patience and sharp eyes, and it is a popular first puzzle for children because the answers are always visible, never hidden by tricks.

On this site

  1. Open any puzzle from the theme list, or make your own.
  2. Press on the first letter of a word and drag to the last letter. The letters you pass are highlighted.
  3. When you lift your finger or mouse, the word is checked. If it is on the list, it stays highlighted and its name is struck off the list.
  4. A word can be found forwards or backwards: dragging from the end to the start also works.
  5. Press Show answers if you are stuck, or New puzzle for a different arrangement of the same words.

Ways to find words faster

  • Look for the rare letters. A Q, Z, X or J in a word tells you where to look. Find the unusual letter in the grid first and check the letters around it.
  • Look for the first letter and then the second. Scan the grid for the first letter of a word. Each time you find one, look at its eight neighbours for the second letter.
  • Work on the longest words first. There are fewer places a long word can fit, and finding it also reveals which letters are used.
  • Find the words of one kind together. If the list has many words with the same ending, such as -ING, look for that ending throughout the grid.
  • Cross off as you go. Strike through the word on the list as soon as you find it, so you do not search twice.

The three levels

Easy puzzles hide words across and down only. Medium puzzles add diagonals. Hard puzzles also allow words to be written backwards. Read more in the levels explained.

On paper

Press Print for a clean sheet with the grid and the list. Use a pencil to circle each word, and keep the answers page for the end.
