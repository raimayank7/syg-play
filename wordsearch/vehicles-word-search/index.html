Vehicles word search

Things that carry us from place to place on land, in the air and on water. Short and familiar words, and clues that tell the child where each vehicle goes.

About this puzzle

This puzzle has 14 words on the theme "vehicles", suited to ages 5 and up. The longest word is HELICOPTER (10 letters) and the shortest is BUS (3 letters); together the words use 89 letters. In the default 12 by 12 grid, which has 144 squares, they fill about 56% of the grid before the filler letters are added, so every new puzzle is a different arrangement of the same words.

Ways to use it

After the puzzle, ask the class to sort the words into land, water and air. Press New puzzle for another arrangement, or change the size and level above. Press Print for a clean sheet with the grid and the word list.

What the letters tell you

In this list the most common letters are R (10), T (9), C (9), E (9), A (8). When you scan the grid, these are the letters you will see most often inside the hidden words, so they are good places to start. The words by length: 1 of 3 letters, 2 of 4 letters, 3 of 5 letters, 1 of 6 letters, 3 of 7 letters, 1 of 8 letters, 2 of 9 letters, 1 of 10 letters. Long words are the easiest to spot once you have the first two letters, and the short ones are the best places to hide, so leave them for last. A spelling challenge: cover the word column of the table below and try to write each word from its clue alone before you check.
