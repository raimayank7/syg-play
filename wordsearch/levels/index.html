Word Search

Word search levels explained

Every puzzle on this site can be set to three levels. The difference is the directions in which words may be hidden. The levels are simple to state and make a big difference to how hard the puzzle feels.

The three levels

LevelDirectionsSuits
EasyLeft to right and top to bottom (2 directions)Young children, new readers
MediumAlso two diagonals, down-right and up-right (4 directions)Most children over eight and adults
HardAll eight directions, including backwards (8 directions)Confident readers and puzzle fans

Why hard is much harder

Reading a word backwards or upwards is unnatural. The eye does not expect it, and many people look only in the natural direction first. Doubling the directions from four to eight does not double the difficulty but it makes a larger difference than any other setting, because half of the hidden words now run against the usual reading direction.

How many places can a word hide?

For a six-letter word, the number of different places and directions in which it could be hidden grows quickly with the level and the size of the grid.

GridEasyMediumHard
10 by 10100150300
12 by 12168266532
15 by 153005001000

So a hard puzzle on a 15 by 15 grid has about 10 times as many hiding places for a word as an easy puzzle on a 10 by 10 grid.

Grid size matters too

A bigger grid has more squares to scan. A 10 by 10 grid has 100 squares, a 12 by 12 has 144 and a 15 by 15 has 225. The play area on a phone keeps the squares tappable by sizing them to the screen, so a 15 by 15 grid has small squares on a small phone. Use 10 or 12 on a small screen, and the bigger sizes for print or a tablet.

Mixing levels

In a class, give the same words at different levels: easy for those who need support, hard for those who finish early. They all learn the same words and nobody is left out.

Clue mode

On the themed puzzles, tick "Clue mode" to replace each word on the list by its clue and the number of letters. It turns the puzzle into a guessing game as well, and works at any level. See the themes.
