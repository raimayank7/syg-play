Weather word search

Words of the sky and the seasons. A rainy-day puzzle that practises words every child needs for a weather chart at school.

About this puzzle

This puzzle has 14 words on the theme "weather", suited to ages 6 and up. The longest word is LIGHTNING (9 letters) and the shortest is FOG (3 letters); together the words use 78 letters. In the default 12 by 12 grid, which has 144 squares, they fill about 49% of the grid before the filler letters are added, so every new puzzle is a different arrangement of the same words.

Ways to use it

Fill in a weather chart for a week and use words from this puzzle. Press New puzzle for another arrangement, or change the size and level above. Press Print for a clean sheet with the grid and the word list.

What the letters tell you

In this list the most common letters are N (12), O (8), I (7), R (6), U (5). When you scan the grid, these are the letters you will see most often inside the hidden words, so they are good places to start. The words by length: 1 of 3 letters, 2 of 4 letters, 5 of 5 letters, 2 of 6 letters, 3 of 7 letters, 1 of 9 letters. Long words are the easiest to spot once you have the first two letters, and the short ones are the best places to hide, so leave them for last. A spelling challenge: cover the word column of the table below and try to write each word from its clue alone before you check.
