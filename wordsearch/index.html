A puzzle for every age

Word searches are among the most loved quiet puzzles: easy to start, satisfying to finish and good for spelling. The themed puzzles here have 14 words with clues, and you can choose easy, medium or hard. Teachers use them for vocabulary, parents for car trips, and grandparents because they like something to do with a pencil.

Questions people ask

Is it free?

Yes. The puzzles and the maker are free, with no login. Nothing you type is stored by us.

Can I print the puzzles?

Yes. Press Print on any puzzle for a clean sheet with the grid and the word list. Press Show answers first if you want a key.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Press on the first letter of a word and drag across it. On a small phone choose a 10 by 10 grid, so the squares stay easy to touch.

Are the words checked?

The puzzle checks that every word is in the grid. The word lists and clues for each theme are written for this site and state plain facts.
