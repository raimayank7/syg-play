Vegetables word search

Vegetables for the kitchen: potato, onion, spinach and others. The words are the ones you hear in a Sunday market, with clues about the shape, colour or part of the plant we eat.

About this puzzle

This puzzle has 14 words on the theme "vegetables", suited to ages 6 and up. The longest word is CAULIFLOWER (11 letters) and the shortest is PEAS (4 letters); together the words use 90 letters. In the default 12 by 12 grid, which has 144 squares, they fill about 56% of the grid before the filler letters are added, so every new puzzle is a different arrangement of the same words.

Ways to use it

Ask children to sort the words into roots, leaves, fruits and seeds. Is a tomato a fruit? Many people say so; start the discussion. Press New puzzle for another arrangement, or change the size and level above. Press Print for a clean sheet with the grid and the word list.

What the letters tell you

In this list the most common letters are A (12), O (9), R (8), I (7), C (7). When you scan the grid, these are the letters you will see most often inside the hidden words, so they are good places to start. The words by length: 2 of 4 letters, 1 of 5 letters, 5 of 6 letters, 4 of 7 letters, 1 of 8 letters, 1 of 11 letters. Long words are the easiest to spot once you have the first two letters, and the short ones are the best places to hide, so leave them for last. A spelling challenge: cover the word column of the table below and try to write each word from its clue alone before you check.
