Word Search

Word search maker

Type your words, one on each line, and get a puzzle you can play on the screen or print. Good for spelling lists, party games, classroom vocabulary and family quizzes.

How to use the maker

  1. Type or paste your words, one per line. Words must be three letters or longer and no longer than the grid.
  2. Press Make my word search. The maker chooses a grid size, which you can change.
  3. Choose the level: easy, medium or hard.
  4. Play it on the screen by dragging across letters, or press Print for a clean sheet.

What happens to your words

Your list is used only in your browser to build the grid. Nothing is sent to us, so you can use names of children in your class or family.

Tips

  • Start with 8 to 12 words in a 12 by 12 grid, and add more once you are comfortable.
  • Names with spaces such as ICE CREAM become one word (ICECREAM), so you may prefer to use single words.
  • If a word does not fit, the maker says so: try a bigger grid or fewer long words.

For advice on a puzzle that is fair and fun, read how to make a good word search.
