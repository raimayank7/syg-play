Farm and village word search

Animals, crops and tools of the farm. A good puzzle for children who live in cities and want to know what a village looks like, and for village children to see their own world in a game.

About this puzzle

This puzzle has 14 words on the theme "farm and village", suited to ages 5 and up. The longest word is SUGARCANE (9 letters) and the shortest is COW (3 letters); together the words use 81 letters. In the default 12 by 12 grid, which has 144 squares, they fill about 51% of the grid before the filler letters are added, so every new puzzle is a different arrangement of the same words.

Ways to use it

Ask each child to say which of the fourteen words they have seen for themselves. Press New puzzle for another arrangement, or change the size and level above. Press Print for a clean sheet with the grid and the word list.

What the letters tell you

In this list the most common letters are A (11), E (8), R (7), C (7), T (6). When you scan the grid, these are the letters you will see most often inside the hidden words, so they are good places to start. The words by length: 2 of 3 letters, 3 of 4 letters, 2 of 5 letters, 1 of 6 letters, 3 of 7 letters, 1 of 8 letters, 2 of 9 letters. Long words are the easiest to spot once you have the first two letters, and the short ones are the best places to hide, so leave them for last. A spelling challenge: cover the word column of the table below and try to write each word from its clue alone before you check.
