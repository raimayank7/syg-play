Darts 301 counter

Enter the total of each three-dart visit. The counter takes it off the player's score, handles busts and suggests a finish.

How 301 works

Each player starts on 301. Players take turns to throw three darts and subtract the total from their score. The first to reach exactly zero wins the leg. In the usual rules the last dart must land in a double (the narrow outer ring) or the bull, which counts as a double 25. The tick box lets you switch off that rule for a casual game.

Busts

If a visit would take a player below zero, or to exactly one when double-out is on (because no double makes one), the visit is a bust: the score goes back to what it was before the visit and play passes on. The counter does this for you and says so.

Suggested finishes

When a score is 170 or lower the counter shows one way to finish in at most three darts, taken from our checkout chart. It is only one route: many scores have several, and you may prefer one that leaves a double you are good at.

Mistakes

Typed a wrong score? Press Undo to take the last visit back. Names can be edited by tapping them. For three or more players, use the general score sheet or play a team format.
