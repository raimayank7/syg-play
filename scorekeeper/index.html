Scorekeeper for game night

Score sheets, a darts counter and a bowling scorer. Nothing to install, nothing sent anywhere.

  • General score sheet Any game, up to 8 players
  • Hearts Low score wins, to 100
  • Pool rummy 101 Out above 101
  • Pool rummy 201 Out above 201
  • Darts 501 counter Busts and finish hints
  • Darts 301 counter Same, shorter
  • Darts checkout chart 2 to 170, computed
  • Bowling scorer Ten frames

Why this one

The sheets keep your scores as you type, so a phone call or a closed tab does not lose a game. The darts chart and bowling examples are computed by program, so the numbers are right. Everything runs in your browser.

Questions

Are my scores saved?

Score sheets are saved in your own browser as you type. Nothing is sent to us.

Is the darts chart correct?

It is generated by trying every combination of darts, and the seven scores it cannot finish match the known list.

Can I print a sheet?

Yes. Each score sheet has a Print button.
