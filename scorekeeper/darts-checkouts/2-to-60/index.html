Darts checkouts from 2 to 60

A checkout is the last visit of a leg: the darts that take the score exactly to zero, with the last dart in a double or the bull. This chart shows one route for each score from 2 to 60, worked out by a program that tries every combination of darts. It always uses the fewest darts, and when there is a choice it begins with the highest-scoring darts.

In this range 21 scores finish with one dart, 38 with two darts and 0 need three. Letters show the ring: S for single, D for double, T for treble, and Bull for the double bull (50). The outer bull (25) is also written 25.

Using the chart

The route here is a standard route, not the only one. Players choose their route to leave themselves a double they like: if you are strong on double 16, you might leave 32 rather than 40. Our darts counter uses this chart for its suggestions. See also the full list and the scores that cannot be finished.
