201 Pool Rummy Score Sheet

A score sheet for 201 pool rummy: players are out when their total goes above 201, and the last player standing wins. The sheet marks who is out.

How to use it

  1. Enter each round's points after every deal: 0 for the winner, card points for everyone else.
  2. A total above 201 means the player is out.
  3. When only one player is left, the game ends.

Saved on your device

The longer limit of 201 makes a longer game than the 101 pool, and the earlier a player is out, the sooner a new player can sometimes re-enter if your group allows it. Our sheet does not allow re-entry automatically: write down the re-entry score yourself if you use that rule.
