Tijori questions and answers

Short answers about the game and the site. The full rules, with an example game, are on how to play.

What is Tijori?

A daily word puzzle made for this site. A five-letter word is locked in a safe (tijori is the Hindi word for a safe). You have 8 tries to find it. After each guess, letters that are right and in the right place lock into the dials, and you are told how many of your other letters belong elsewhere in the word.

What does "+2 elsewhere" mean?

Two of the letters in your guess, other than the locked ones, are in the secret word but not in the places where you put them. The game does not say which two. Finding out is the puzzle.

What does a result of 0 mean?

None of the five letters you guessed is anywhere in the word. The keyboard greys them out. It is a useful result, because five letters are ruled out at once.

How are repeated letters counted?

A letter counts only as many times as it occurs in the secret word. If the word has one T and your guess has two, at most one of them is counted. A locked letter is not counted again as "elsewhere".

Can the answer have the same letter twice?

Yes. Words such as HAPPY and SPEED can be answers.

Why was my word refused?

Guesses must be in our word list, a general English list of about six and a half thousand five-letter words. Names, abbreviations and some very new or rare words are not in it. A refused word does not use up a try.

Can a plural be the answer?

No. Plurals and verb forms made by adding S, and past forms in ED, are accepted as guesses but are never the answer. The answers are 807 common words.

When does the new puzzle appear?

At midnight by the clock of your own device. Everyone gets the same word on the same date.

I missed a day. Can I still play it?

Yes, on the Earlier days page. Choose the date. Archive games do not count towards your streak.

How does the hint work?

Once per game, the Hint button opens the first dial that is still shut. Your shared result will say "with a hint".

What is Mark letters for?

It lets you keep notes on the keyboard. With marking on, tapping a key greys it (not in the word), rings it (in the word) or clears it, instead of typing.

Where are my results kept?

In your browser on your device. They are not sent to us, and they are lost if you clear the browser data or change device.

What does the Share button send?

One plain sentence with the puzzle number, the date and how many tries you needed. It never contains the word or your guesses.

Is this the same as other daily word games?

No. Tijori is our own design, with its own name, rules and look. In particular it does not colour each letter; it locks the letters that are in place and gives only a count for the rest, in the tradition of old pen-and-paper guessing games. It is not connected with any newspaper or company.

Is there a Hindi version?

Yes. The Hindi Tijori uses everyday three-akshar words and has its own keyboard and rules page.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is free, there is no account and nothing to install. Advertisements on the guide pages pay for the site; the game pages themselves carry no advertisements.
