Which letters go where in five-letter words

In Tijori a letter in the right place locks, so it pays to know where letters like to stand. This table counts, for each letter, how many of the 807 answer words have it as their first, second, third, fourth and fifth letter. The second column is the share of answer words that contain the letter at all. For each letter, its most frequent position is highlighted.

The commonest letter in each place

Taking each position by itself, the letter found there most often is: position 1: S (139 words), position 2: R (114 words), position 3: A (135 words), position 4: E (103 words), position 5: E (188 words). A word built from those five letters in those places does not exist, but the list tells you what a "typical" five-letter word looks like: a consonant, a vowel or R or L, a vowel, a consonant or E, and a final E, T or Y.

First letters

The most frequent first letters are S (139), C (71), A (59), T (58), B (52), F (49), P (46), M (36). Vowels are scarce at the start: common five-letter words nearly always open with a consonant, and very often with a pair such as ST, SH, CH, TR, BR, CR, GR, PL, CL or SP.

Last letters

The most frequent last letters are E (188), T (103), Y (83), R (75), H (60), D (53), L (49), N (49). E leads because of the silent E (PLACE, STONE, WHITE); Y because of adjectives and adverbs (HAPPY, EARLY); T, R, D, H, L and N close most of the rest. Remember that plurals in S are never answers in Tijori, so S is far rarer at the end here than in a dictionary.

Vowels

Counting only A, E, I, O and U: 250 answer words have exactly one vowel, 474 have two, 81 have three, 2 have four or more and 0 have none (they use Y instead). So two vowels is the usual number, and a word with three is common enough that you should not rule it out. The second and third places hold most of the vowels; the fourth place is shared between vowels and consonants.

Repeated letters

234 of the 807 answers (29%) contain some letter more than once. That is too many to ignore. If you have four letters placed or proved and nothing fits, ask whether one of them appears twice. More on this in tricky words.

The rare letters

The five least common letters in the answers are V, Q, Z, X, J. Do not spend an early guess testing them. If the common letters run out and the word still will not come, then think of them, and of where they sit: Q before U, X near the end, J and Z mostly at the start, V before a vowel.

How to use the table in a game

  • Choosing a guess. Between two words that test the same letters, play the one that has them in their likelier places. You gain a better chance of a lock at no cost.
  • Reading "+1 elsewhere". When you know a letter is in the word but not where, try it next in its most frequent position that you have not yet ruled out.
  • Finishing. With three dials locked, run through the likely letters for each open place from the table instead of the whole alphabet.

The counts are made from the Tijori answer list when the site is built. Lists of all English five-letter words, which include plurals and rare words, give somewhat different figures, especially for S. See also the best starting words.
