Earlier days: the archive

Pick a date to play the puzzle of that day. Archive games do not change your daily streak.

    How the archive works

    Every date from 1 November 2026 onward has its own Tijori puzzle. Choose a date in the box above the safe and that day's word is set behind the dials, exactly as it was for everyone who played it then. The box opens on yesterday, since that is the puzzle people most often miss. Dates in the future cannot be chosen.

    A game you start in the archive is saved in your browser under its date, so you can leave a half-played puzzle and come back to it. If you have already played that date, the page shows your finished game.

    What counts and what does not

    Only the puzzle of today's date, played on the home page, counts towards your results and your streak. Archive games are for enjoyment and practice. This keeps the streak honest: it shows on how many days in a row you actually came and opened the safe.

    Uses for the archive

    • Catching up. Back from a trip? Play the days you missed, in order.
    • Playing with someone in another time zone. A new puzzle appears at midnight on each player's own device. If a friend abroad is a day ahead of or behind you, agree on a date and both choose it here.
    • A group challenge. Pick a date nobody in the group has played, and everybody starts together. See playing as a group.
    • Second attempts. If a puzzle beat you, wait a few days until you have forgotten the word, then try it again from the archive.
    • Showing a newcomer. Open an old puzzle you remember and talk a friend through it without spoiling today's.

    The puzzle number

    Each puzzle has a number as well as a date: No. 1 was 1 November 2026, and the number goes up by one each day. The number appears above the safe and in the sentence you share, so that friends can be sure they are comparing the same puzzle.

    Will old puzzles ever change?

    The word for a date is fixed by its place in the game's list. We do not intend to change the list. If a word ever has to be removed because it turns out to be unsuitable, the words for the days after it would shift; we would do that only for a good reason, and say so on the questions page.

    For unlimited random puzzles instead of dated ones, use practice mode.
