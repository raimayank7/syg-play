Tijori: crack today's five-letter word safe

One secret word a day, the same for everyone. Letters in the right place lock into the dials. You have 8 tries.

    How Tijori works

    Tijori is the Hindi word for a safe. Behind the five dials is a five-letter English word. Type a word and press Enter. Then two things happen:

    • Locked. Every letter of your guess that is correct and in the correct place locks into its dial and stays there. You can see exactly which letters these are.
    • Elsewhere. For your other letters you get only a number, such as "+2 elsewhere". It means two more letters of your guess belong to the word, but in different places. The game does not say which two. Working that out is the puzzle.

    If the answer is "0", none of your five letters is in the word, and the keyboard greys them all out for you. That is good news: five letters gone in one move.

    You have eight tries. One hint is allowed: it opens one dial. The Mark letters button lets you grey out or ring letters on the keyboard as you reason, like pencil notes. The full rules, with an example game played move by move, are on how to play.

    Your results

    A puzzle of reasoning, not luck

    Because the game counts your loose letters without pointing at them, every guess is a small experiment. Change one letter from your last guess and watch whether the count goes up or down: now you know something about both letters. Players who enjoy sudoku or number-guessing games will feel at home. Our strategy guide shows the method, and the best starting words are worked out from the game's own word list.

    Good to know

    • A new word appears at midnight on your own device's clock. Everyone gets the same word on the same date.
    • Your game and results stay in your browser. There is no login and nothing to install.
    • Only real words are accepted as guesses. The answers are common words; plurals made by adding S are never answers.
    • Sharing sends one plain sentence with your number of tries. It never gives away the word.
