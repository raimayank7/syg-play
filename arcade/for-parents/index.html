Tiny Arcade

A note for parents

Browser games can be a nuisance for families: adverts that trick children into tapping, chat with strangers, requests to buy extras. These games are made without those things, and this page says exactly what they do.

What the games do not have

  • No login and no account, so we do not know who is playing.
  • No chat, comments or other contact between players.
  • No purchases inside the game and no pretend rewards or timers that push children to come back.
  • No ads inside the games. The game pages have no advertising on the board itself; pages with guides may show advertisements around the text, from Google, as described in our privacy policy.

What they keep

Your scores and settings are kept in your own browser. They are not sent to us. The Share button creates a message that you choose to send yourself.

Using the games well

  • Short sessions of 5 to 15 minutes are best. Decide the time before starting.
  • Play together when you can, especially with young children: take turns, and talk about the moves.
  • Use the learning page of each game for ideas that connect it to schoolwork.

Age

Memory Match and Noughts and Crosses suit children from about five. Snake and Join Tiles suit children from about seven. Safe Squares is best from eight or nine, and is a good challenge for teenagers and adults.

Questions parents ask

  • Do the games ask for permissions? No. They do not use the camera, microphone, contacts or location.
  • Can my child talk to strangers here? No. There is no chat, comment box or multiplayer mode.
  • Will the games spend my data? Very little. A game page is small, and playing uses no data after it has loaded.
  • What if my child gets frustrated? Every game has an easy setting. Safe Squares has an easy board, and Noughts and Crosses has an easy computer. Praise effort, not scores.
