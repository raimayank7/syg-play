Join Tiles: play free online

Slide numbered tiles on a four-by-four board and join equal ones into bigger numbers.

About the game

Join Tiles is a number puzzle on a four-by-four board. Each move slides every tile as far as it will go in one direction. When two tiles with the same number meet, they join into one tile with double the number. After every move a new small tile appears. The game ends when no move is possible.

Controls

Arrow keys or W, A, S, D on a keyboard. On a phone, swipe anywhere on the board.

How to play Tips What it teaches

A closer look

At the start there are two small tiles on the board. Slide them left and a new one appears. Join two 2s to make a 4, then two 4s to make an 8. Soon you will have a handful of tiles, and the important choice appears: which direction should you slide? A beginner swipes in a different direction each time and watches the big tile wander. A more experienced player picks a corner for the biggest tile and swipes mostly in two directions, so the large tiles stay in place while smaller ones gather next to them. The board rewards patience: a move that joins nothing but keeps the order is often better than a move that scores.

Ways to vary the game

  • Play for a fixed number of moves, say 100, and compare the highest tile you reach.
  • Play with a corner rule: you may never slide towards the corner where your biggest tile is, unless there is no other legal move.
  • Take turns with a friend, each making ten moves on the same board, and see who ruins it less.

Questions

How many different tiles are there?

The game starts with 2 and 4 tiles. Joining makes 8, 16, 32 and so on. The board is small, so very large tiles are rare.

Is there a way to win?

There is no single winning point; the aim is a higher score. Many players aim to make a tile of 2048 or more.

Why does a new tile appear after a move?

It keeps the board filling up, so the game always ends sooner or later.
