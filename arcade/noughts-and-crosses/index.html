Noughts and Crosses: play free online

Play the classic three-in-a-row game against the computer, easy or unbeatable.

About the game

Noughts and crosses, also called tic-tac-toe, is the oldest pencil-and-paper game in many homes. Two players take turns to mark a square in a three-by-three grid, one with X and one with O. The first to make a line of three in a row, column or diagonal wins. Here you play against the computer, which has an easy level and a hard level.

Controls

Tap or click an empty square. There is nothing to press on the keyboard.

How to play Tips What it teaches

A closer look

Suppose you play X and start in the centre. The computer, playing O, must answer in a corner (any other answer loses). Now you can make a threat with your second mark, for example an edge square that lines up with the centre. The computer must block it, and so on: each move is forced until the board fills up in a draw. Only if somebody makes a mistake, such as ignoring a threat, does the game end in a win. That is why the game is so useful for learning: all the strategy is about spotting threats and creating two at once.

Ways to vary the game

  • Play on the easy level and try to win in five moves or fewer: it can be done if you create a fork early.
  • Play on the hard level as O (second) and see whether you can force a draw every time.
  • Draw the board on paper and play against a friend, using the computer to check who played better.

Questions

Can the hard computer be beaten?

No. If you play perfectly it is a draw, and if you make a mistake the computer takes advantage. We tested it over a thousand games, as first and as second player, and it never lost.

Does easy mode ever win?

Sometimes, by luck, if you are careless. It plays a random empty square each turn.

Is my record saved?

Yes, in your browser: how many games you have won, drawn and lost.
