Memory Match: play free online

Flip two cards at a time and find all the pairs in as few moves as you can.

About the game

Memory Match is a card game for one player: a board of face-down cards, each with a twin. You turn two over at a time. If they match, they stay face up; if not, they turn back and you try to remember where they were. The fewer moves you take, the better your score.

Controls

Tap or click a card to turn it over. There is nothing to press on the keyboard.

How to play Tips What it teaches

A closer look

On the small board there are eight pairs. Turn over two cards: say a dog at the top left and a bird in the second row. They do not match, so they turn back, but you now know where a dog and a bird are. Turn over two new cards, and if one is a cat, you have learned a cat; if a card in your next flip is a dog, you can go straight to the top left for the match. Each unmatched flip is not a wasted move but a piece of the map. Good players do not remember everything: they remember the pairs that are half-known and work to complete them.

Ways to vary the game

  • Play against a friend: take turns, and each match scores a point for the player who found it.
  • On the large board, set yourself a goal of finding all the pairs in two sweeps: the first to learn the cards, the second to match them.
  • Try the game after a short break: memory is better when you are fresh, and worse when you are tired, so your score tells you something about the day.

Questions

What is the lowest score possible?

On the small board, if you were lucky enough to pick right every time, 8 moves. In practice, 12 to 18 is a good score; on the large board, 30 to 50.

Do the cards change every game?

Yes. The cards are shuffled every time you press New game.

Is it for children?

Yes, but it is a good test for adults on the large board.
