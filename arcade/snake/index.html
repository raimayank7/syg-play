Snake: play free online

Guide a growing snake round the board, eat the apples and never touch the wall or yourself.

About the game

Snake is one of the simplest and most replayed games ever made. You steer a snake around a small board. Every apple it eats makes it longer and the game a little faster. The only way to lose is to run into a wall or into your own tail, and the only way to win is to keep going longer than last time.

Controls

Arrow keys or W, A, S, D on a keyboard. On a phone, swipe on the board or use the four buttons under it. Space (or the middle button) pauses.

How to play Tips What it teaches

A closer look

Imagine the board as a grid with the snake three squares long in the middle, heading right, and an apple a few squares above it. A beginner heads straight for the apple and eats it. A better player first asks which way the body will lie afterwards. If the snake turns up and then right, its tail will follow the same path, so the squares it just left become a wall behind it. Playing well means leaving yourself a way out at every step. By the time the snake is twenty squares long, you are not steering a short line; you are steering a long road that blocks half of the board.

Ways to vary the game

  • Play with a friend and pass the device after each crash, to see who gets the higher total in three turns.
  • Set yourself a rule: never touch the outer ring of squares until the snake is 15 long. This forces you to plan in the middle of the board.
  • Play with the sound off and your eyes on the apple only, then with your eyes on the tail only: you will see how much of the skill is knowing where you have been.

Questions

How fast does the snake go?

It starts slowly and gets quicker with every apple until it reaches a fixed top speed, so a long game stays playable.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes, in your own browser. It is not sent to us and it disappears if you clear the site data.

Can I pause?

Press space or the pause button in the middle of the pad.
