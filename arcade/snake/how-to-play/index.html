How to play Snake

Snake is one of the simplest and most replayed games ever made. You steer a snake around a small board. Every apple it eats makes it longer and the game a little faster. The only way to lose is to run into a wall or into your own tail, and the only way to win is to keep going longer than last time.

Rules

  1. The snake always moves forward. You choose only its direction at each turn.
  2. Eating the red apple adds one to your score and makes the snake one square longer.
  3. The snake cannot turn straight back on itself: if you are moving right, pressing left does nothing.
  4. The game ends when the head touches a wall or any part of the snake’s body.
  5. The game speeds up slightly with every apple, down to a minimum delay.

Controls

Arrow keys or W, A, S, D on a keyboard. On a phone, swipe on the board or use the four buttons under it. Space (or the middle button) pauses.

Your first game

Open the Snake game and play one round with no plan, just to see how it feels. Notice what ended the game: that tells you what to watch for next time. Then read the tips and play again, using one tip at a time.

Scoring

Scoring is one point for each apple, so your score is simply the number of apples you have eaten. The snake’s length is your score plus three, because it starts three squares long. The game speeds up as the score grows, to a limit, so after about 25 apples the speed stops rising and only the crowding of the board makes it harder. Your best score is kept for this browser. If you want a fair contest with a friend, each of you should play three games and add the scores, because one lucky game says little.

Scores and saving

Your best score is kept in your own browser, on this device only. If you clear the site data or use a different browser, it will start again from zero. You can send a score to a friend with the Share button at the end of the game.

Questions

How fast does the snake go?

It starts slowly and gets quicker with every apple until it reaches a fixed top speed, so a long game stays playable.

Does the game save my best score?

Yes, in your own browser. It is not sent to us and it disappears if you clear the site data.

Can I pause?

Press space or the pause button in the middle of the pad.
