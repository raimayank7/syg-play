Tiny Arcade

Playing on a phone or tablet

All five games work on a touch screen. This page explains the controls, and some tips to make play comfortable.

Controls by game

  • Snake: swipe on the board in the direction you want to go, or use the four buttons under it. The middle button pauses.
  • Memory Match: tap a card to turn it over.
  • Join Tiles: swipe anywhere on the board. Swipe in the direction you want the tiles to slide.
  • Safe Squares: tap to open a square. Tick Flag mode first if you want taps to mark pits instead of opening squares. On a computer, a right click flags a square.
  • Noughts and Crosses: tap an empty square.

Tips for touch screens

  • Hold the phone upright for snake and join tiles, and sideways for the large memory board if you want bigger cards.
  • If the page scrolls when you swipe on the board, swipe a little more firmly; the board is set to catch swipes, but a very short touch counts as a tap.
  • Turn on rotation lock if the screen keeps turning while you play.
  • On a small phone, choose the small or easy level so that squares and cards stay large enough to tap.

Sound and data

The games have no sound and use very little data. They work again offline once the page has loaded, as far as your browser allows. Your scores stay on your device.

Common problems

  • The page moves when I swipe: swipe on the board itself, not next to it. Our boards are set to catch swipes.
  • The squares are too small: choose the easy level of Safe Squares or the small board of Memory Match.
  • My score disappeared: scores live in your browser. Private windows and cleared site data remove them.
  • The game feels slow: close other tabs and apps. The games are light, but old phones with many apps open can be sluggish.
