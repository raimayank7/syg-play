Tiny Arcade: small games for a short break

Five original games you can play in a minute: snake, memory match, join tiles, safe squares and noughts and crosses. Free, no login, and they work on a phone.

  • Snake A game of reflexes and planning
  • Memory Match A game of memory and patience
  • Join Tiles A number puzzle that is easy to start and hard to master
  • Safe Squares A game of logic and careful thought
  • Noughts and Crosses A classic for two, now against the computer

Quick, calm and original

Each game takes seconds to learn and a lifetime to master. They are made for the short breaks of a day: between lessons, on a bus, while the tea cools. There are no adverts on the playing board, no sign-in and no chat. Read the note for parents or how the games are made.

Questions people ask

Are the games free?

Yes, completely. There is no login, no download and nothing to buy.

Do they work on a phone?

Yes. You can swipe or tap, and the board fits the screen.

Are my scores saved?

Your best scores are saved in your own browser on your own device. We do not receive them.

Are the games copied from other games?

No. They are written for this site. The rules are the classic rules of each type of game.
