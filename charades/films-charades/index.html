Hindi film titles charades words

Acting out a film title is the classic charades challenge. The titles on this list are widely known Hindi films. Split each title into words and act them one at a time, or find a way to act the whole idea at once.

How hard is this list?

Level: Hard. It suits adults and teens who know the subject well. The list has 35 entries, of which 14 are single words and 21 are phrases of two words or more. The longest is "Dilwale Dulhania Le Jayenge". Phrases take longer to act than single words, so a list with many of them makes a slower and funnier game.

Acting tips for this list

Show the number of words with your fingers, then act word by word. Signs that guessers know: touching your ear for "sounds like", a pretend film camera for "film".

How to play

Choose two teams. A player from the first team opens the game page, picks "Hindi film titles", and presses Start a turn. They act each word silently while the team guesses, and tap Got it or Pass. At the end of the time the page shows the score, which you can send to the group. Then the other team takes a turn. For printed slips, press Copy the words, paste them into a document and cut them up. More in the rules and how to act.
