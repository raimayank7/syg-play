Two classic party games

Charades needs a few words and a team; antakshari needs a few songs and some courage. Both are loud, silly and perfect for gatherings with people of mixed ages. This site gives hosts the parts that are hard to remember in the moment: the word lists, a timer, the rules, and a fair way to pick letters.

Questions people ask

Do I need to pay or log in?

No. The game and the word lists are free, with no account.

Does the game record my voice or camera?

No. It uses only your screen and a timer. Nothing is sent to us.

Can I use my own words?

Copy any list from its page, add your own words, write them on slips of paper and play the classic way.

Does the antakshari page have songs?

No. It has the rules, a letter picker and a score counter. The songs are yours.
