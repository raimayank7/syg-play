Antakshari ideas and variations

Plain antakshari is great, but a few twists make it fresh for a family that plays often. All of these work with the same rules and the same letter picker.

Theme rounds

  • Decade round: every song must come from the same decade, chosen by the host.
  • Language round: only Punjabi songs, only old Hindi film songs, only devotional songs.
  • Movie round: every song must be from a film with a particular theme, such as rain or train journeys.
  • Duet round: the song must be sung as a duet by two people from the same team.

Different ways of singing

  • Hum round: the team hums the song and the other team must name it before starting their own.
  • One-line round: only one line each, which keeps the game quick.
  • Action round: sing while acting out the song, which combines antakshari with charades.

Include everyone

  • Pair each child with an adult, so children can sing with help.
  • Give grandparents the "old songs" round, so they are the experts.
  • Let anybody ask for one "lifeline" when stuck: a team member may whisper the first words.

A sample evening

RoundRule
1. Warm-upAny song, one verse, no scoring
2. Last letterClassic rules, 15 seconds per turn
3. Old songsOnly songs from a decade chosen by the host
4. Hum roundHum the song, the other team names it, then sings the next
5. FinaleBoth teams sing together as one

On a journey

In a car or a bus, use the last letter rule with the alphabet written on paper, and let the oldest child keep the score. Use the picker once at the start to choose a letter, and after that, rely on the last-letter rule. Stop the game while the driver needs to concentrate, and take turns so no one gets tired.

Fair play

Keep a list of songs already used, ask the other team to accept a song before you count it, and let one person act as a judge for disputed songs. For the rules, see how to play antakshari.
