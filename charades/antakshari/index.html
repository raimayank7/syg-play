Antakshari rules: how to play

Antakshari is the Indian party game in which teams sing songs one after another, and the next song must begin with the letter the previous song ended with. It is played on long journeys, at weddings and at family gatherings, and needs only a good memory and some courage. This page gives the rules in a form that works for any group. We do not list songs or lyrics: you bring the songs.

The basic game

  1. Split into two teams, and choose which team starts.
  2. The first team picks a letter (our letter picker can choose for them) and sings a song that begins with it.
  3. When the song ends, the other team must sing a song beginning with the last letter of the song just sung.
  4. Teams take turns. A team that cannot start a song in the time allowed (15 seconds is usual) loses that turn, and the other team may take it.
  5. The team with the most points when the time is up wins.

Details your group should agree

  • A song or a verse? Most groups sing a few lines, called a mukhda, and stop when the next team can take over. Agree on the minimum length.
  • Which languages? Hindi film songs, old songs, Punjabi songs, devotional songs: agree on a rule so one team does not surprise the other.
  • Can a song be used twice? The usual rule is no. Keep a list of songs already sung.
  • What about hard letters? Some letters of the Hindi alphabet rarely begin songs. A common fix is that the next team may start with the last letter or the letter just before it.

Scoring

One point for each song. Many groups give a bonus point for singing a complete verse, or for a song nobody else knew. For a quiet game, give points only for songs that the other team accepts as real.

Tools

Use the letter picker, which has a timer and a score counter for two teams. For themes and ideas, see antakshari ideas.
