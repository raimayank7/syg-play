Feelings and moods charades words

Feelings are harder to act out than objects, because they live in the face and body. This category is good practice in expression and a favourite with teens and adults who enjoy performing.

How hard is this list?

Level: Medium. It suits teens and adults, or children who have played before. The list has 30 entries, of which 30 are single words and 0 are phrases of two words or more. The longest is "embarrassed". Phrases take longer to act than single words, so a list with many of them makes a slower and funnier game.

Acting tips for this list

Use your face first, then your whole body. A person who is nervous bites nails; a person who is proud stands tall. Making a small scene helps: you have just received good or bad news.

How to play

Choose two teams. A player from the first team opens the game page, picks "Feelings and moods", and presses Start a turn. They act each word silently while the team guesses, and tap Got it or Pass. At the end of the time the page shows the score, which you can send to the group. Then the other team takes a turn. For printed slips, press Copy the words, paste them into a document and cut them up. More in the rules and how to act.
