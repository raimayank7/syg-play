Indian foods charades words

Foods everyone has eaten make good charades words, especially when the way of eating is part of the fun: rolling out a roti, stirring a pot, blowing on hot tea. A good category for family gatherings.

How hard is this list?

Level: Easy. It suits children from about six, and adults who want a relaxed round. The list has 35 entries, of which 28 are single words and 7 are phrases of two words or more. The longest is "sugarcane juice". Phrases take longer to act than single words, so a list with many of them makes a slower and funnier game.

Acting tips for this list

Act how it is made or eaten. For a dosa, spread batter in a circle; for a laddoo, roll sweet dough between your palms; for chai, pour from a height.

How to play

Choose two teams. A player from the first team opens the game page, picks "Indian foods", and presses Start a turn. They act each word silently while the team guesses, and tap Got it or Pass. At the end of the time the page shows the score, which you can send to the group. Then the other team takes a turn. For printed slips, press Copy the words, paste them into a document and cut them up. More in the rules and how to act.
