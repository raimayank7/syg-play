Stories and characters charades words

How hard is this list?

Level: Medium. It suits teens and adults, or children who have played before. The list has 30 entries, of which 10 are single words and 20 are phrases of two words or more. The longest is "monkey and crocodile". Phrases take longer to act than single words, so a list with many of them makes a slower and funnier game.

Acting tips for this list

Pick the most famous moment of the story: the crow dropping pebbles into a pot, the hare sleeping beside the road. Act as the character and as the thing he does.

How to play

Choose two teams. A player from the first team opens the game page, picks "Stories and characters", and presses Start a turn. They act each word silently while the team guesses, and tap Got it or Pass. At the end of the time the page shows the score, which you can send to the group. Then the other team takes a turn. For printed slips, press Copy the words, paste them into a document and cut them up. More in the rules and how to act.
