Charades

Play charades

Pick a category and a time. The actor holds the phone and acts out each word without speaking; the team guesses. Tap Got it or Pass, and see the score at the end of the turn.

How it works

  1. Choose a category: animals, actions, jobs, foods, films and more.
  2. Choose how long a turn lasts. Sixty seconds is the usual time.
  3. Press Start a turn. The first word appears.
  4. The actor acts out the word silently. When the team guesses it, tap Got it. If it is too hard, tap Pass.
  5. When the time is up, the page shows the words guessed and passed, and lets you share the score.

Notes

The actor holds the phone so that only they can see the word. Words come in a random order and none repeats in a turn. If the deck runs out before the time, the passed words come back. For the full rules see how to play.
