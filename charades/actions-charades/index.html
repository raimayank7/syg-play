Actions and verbs charades words

Verbs are what charades is really made of: you act them out one at a time. These everyday actions need no props and can be done in a small room. They also work as a warm-up for harder categories.

How hard is this list?

Level: Easy. It suits children from about six, and adults who want a relaxed round. The list has 34 entries, of which 20 are single words and 14 are phrases of two words or more. The longest is "waiting for a bus". Phrases take longer to act than single words, so a list with many of them makes a slower and funnier game.

Acting tips for this list

Make the action large and slow. If the word is an action done to something, such as peeling, act the object too. Do not say the word aloud, even if it is on the tip of your tongue.

How to play

Choose two teams. A player from the first team opens the game page, picks "Actions and verbs", and presses Start a turn. They act each word silently while the team guesses, and tap Got it or Pass. At the end of the time the page shows the score, which you can send to the group. Then the other team takes a turn. For printed slips, press Copy the words, paste them into a document and cut them up. More in the rules and how to act.
